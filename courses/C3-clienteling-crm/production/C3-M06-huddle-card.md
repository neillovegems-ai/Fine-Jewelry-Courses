# C3-M06 Morning Huddle Card: VIP Tiering and Time Allocation

## Core Principle
"If you treat every client identically, you are neglecting your most valuable collectors. Allocate 60% of your proactive clienteling time to the top 20% of your book."

## Key Focus Points (5-Minute Team Sync)
1. **Know Your Tier 1 VICs:** These are your top 10–15 clients who drive the majority of your commission and repeat volume. They deserve private salon viewings, bespoke atelier sourcing, and hand-delivered pieces.
2. **Protect the Morning 45:** The first 45 minutes of your shift is sacred clienteling time. Do not check personal email or browse social media—focus exclusively on high-value client touches.
3. **Tier 2 Nurturing:** Ensure your Core clients are actively progressing through the 2-2-2 cadence so they naturally ascend into Tier 1 status.

## Daily Floor Challenge
Audit your client book. Identify your top 10 VICs. Review their records to ensure their anniversary dates, metal preferences, and primary wish-list pieces are 100% complete!
