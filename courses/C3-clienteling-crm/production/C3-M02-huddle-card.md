# C3-M02 Morning Huddle Card: Data Capture That Feels Like Service

## Core Principle
"Never ask for contact information to put someone on a 'mailing list.' Ask for their details to protect their jewelry, safeguard their warranty, and remember their taste."

## Key Focus Points (5-Minute Team Sync)
1. **Kill the Cash-Wrap Ask:** Never wait until the client is holding their credit card to ask: "What is your email address?" That is when buyer vigilance is highest.
2. **The Custodial Service Bridge:** Anchor data capture to service: "Let me log your finger size and cleaning schedule so whenever your partner visits, we ensure any milestone gift fits perfectly without guessing."
3. **Capture Three Pillars:** On every interaction today, aim to discover at least three profiling pillars: preferred contact channel, next major milestone, and ring size.

## Daily Floor Challenge
During at least three client conversations today, introduce the "Private Collection Dossier" concept and achieve an 85%+ data capture rate using service-first language.
