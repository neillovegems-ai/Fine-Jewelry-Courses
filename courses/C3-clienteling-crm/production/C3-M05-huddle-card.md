# C3-M05 Morning Huddle Card: Writing Outreach That Gets Replies

## Core Principle
"A client does not ignore your message because they are busy. They ignore your message because it reads like it was sent to 5,000 other people. Make it undeniably 1-to-1."

## Key Focus Points (5-Minute Team Sync)
1. **The 4-Part SMS Blueprint:** (1) Warm Personal Greeting &rarr; (2) Specific Personal Memory Anchor &rarr; (3) Styled Daylight Photo/Video &rarr; (4) Low-Friction Question.
2. **Never Say 'Check In' or 'Follow Up':** These words scream "salesperson." Replace them with: "I was inspecting a newly arrived sapphire from our workshop this morning and immediately thought of you..."
3. **Always Include Styled Media:** An image of a diamond ring resting on dark velvet or on a hand generates 3x more replies than text alone.

## Daily Floor Challenge
Draft and send three 1-to-1 styled photo WhatsApp/SMS messages to clients with pending wish-list items before 2:00 PM today.
