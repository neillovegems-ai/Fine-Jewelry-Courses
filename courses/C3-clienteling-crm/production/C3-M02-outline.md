# C3-M02 Course Outline: Data Capture That Feels Like Service

## 1. Metadata
- **Course:** C3 Clienteling & CRM: Building a Client Book That Compounds
- **Module:** 2
- **Title:** Data Capture That Feels Like Service
- **Track:** Foundations
- **Target Personas:** P1 (New Associate), P2 (Maison Client Advisor), P3 (Cruise/Travel Specialist)
- **Estimated Study Time:** 45–60 minutes
- **Core KPIs:** Data Capture Rate (Target: 85%+), Profile Completeness Score, Client Opt-In Rate

## 2. Learning Objectives
1. Identify the psychological failure points of transactional point-of-sale data collection ("Can I get your email?").
2. Master the 7-Pillar Client Profiling Matrix to systematically record high-value preferences.
3. Apply conversational elicitation techniques to capture milestone dates, ring sizes, and aesthetic tastes mid-conversation.
4. Position data stewardship as a custodial luxury service rather than an administrative marketing requirement.

## 3. Structural Breakdown
- **Section 1:** The Checkout Interrogation Trap (Why 80% of clients decline).
- **Section 2:** The 7-Pillar Luxury Client Profiling Matrix.
- **Section 3:** In-Conversation Organic Capture Techniques (The cleaning bridge, the styling tray).
- **Section 4:** Privacy, Permission, and Discretion (Building trust with HNW individuals).
- **Section 5:** Verbatim Floor Scripts for Common Hesitations.
- **Section 6:** Actionable Floor Drills & Weekly Practice Protocol.
