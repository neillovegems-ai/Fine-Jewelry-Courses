# C3-M06 Knowledge Assessment: VIP Tiering and Time Allocation

   - A) 15%
   - B) 35%
   - C) 70% to 80% [CORRECT]
   - D) Exactly 100%

2. **What is the primary danger of treating all clients in your book with identical time allocation?**

   - D) A customer who has made exactly one purchase under $500.

4. **What percentage of an associate's proactive clienteling time should be dedicated to Tier 1 and Tier 2 clients?**
   - A) Under 20%
   - B) Exactly 33%
   - C) Approximately 60% to 70% [CORRECT]
