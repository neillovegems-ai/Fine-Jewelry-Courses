# C3-M05 Course Outline: Writing Outreach That Gets Replies (Text, Email, Handwritten)

## 1. Metadata
- **Course:** C3 Clienteling & CRM: Building a Client Book That Compounds
- **Module:** 5
- **Title:** Writing Outreach That Gets Replies (Text, Email, Handwritten)
- **Track:** Foundations
- **Target Personas:** P1 (New Associate), P2 (Maison Client Advisor), P3 (Cruise/Travel Specialist)
- **Estimated Study Time:** 45–60 minutes
- **Core KPIs:** Outreach Reply Rate (Target: 45%+), Mobile Message Read Rate, Digital-to-Salon Appointment Conversion

## 2. Learning Objectives
1. Select the correct communication channel based on client profiling preferences and message intent.
2. Draft high-response SMS and WhatsApp messages that feel personal, discrete, and devoid of corporate marketing jargon.
3. Construct luxury storytelling emails that achieve 60%+ open rates among high-net-worth clients.
4. Execute bespoke handwritten stationery protocols that establish lifelong emotional resonance.
5. Capture professional mobile jewelry photography and macro video to accompany digital outreach.

- **Section 5:** The Art of Bespoke Handwritten Stationery.
- **Section 6:** Mobile Jewelry Photography & Video Staging.
