# C3-M03 Course Outline: The 2-2-2 Cadence Explained

## 1. Metadata
- **Course:** C3 Clienteling & CRM: Building a Client Book That Compounds
- **Module:** 3
- **Title:** The 2-2-2 Cadence Explained
- **Track:** Foundations
- **Target Personas:** P1 (New Associate), P2 (Maison Client Advisor), P3 (Cruise/Travel Specialist)
- **Estimated Study Time:** 45–60 minutes
- **Core KPIs:** 2-2-2 Cadence Completion Rate (Target: 95%+), Client Response Rate, Repeat Appointment Velocity

## 2. Learning Objectives
1. Understand the psychological decay curve of post-purchase excitement and combat buyer's remorse.
2. Master the exact timing, channels, and objectives of the 2-Day, 2-Week, and 2-Month touchpoints.
3. Write high-impact handwritten gratitude notes that clients keep for years.
4. Convert the 2-Month touchpoint into high-ticket companion jewelry sales without aggressive pitching.

## 3. Structural Breakdown
- **Section 1:** The Post-Purchase Emotional Decay Curve.
- **Section 2:** The 2-Day Gratitude Touchpoint (Handwritten stationery mastery).
- **Section 3:** The 2-Week Custodial Care Touchpoint (Fit, security, appraisal delivery).
- **Section 4:** The 2-Month Curated Horizon Touchpoint (Companion styling & milestone preview).
- **Section 5:** Channel Selection: When to Write, Text, or Call.
- **Section 6:** Actionable Floor Drills & Weekly Practice Protocol.
