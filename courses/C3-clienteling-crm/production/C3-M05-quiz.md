# C3-M05 Knowledge Assessment: Writing Outreach That Gets Replies

1. **Why do mass promotional emails sent by jewelry brands achieve low engagement among luxury buyers?**
   - A) High-net-worth clients immediately recognize impersonal broadcast marketing and delete it without reading. [CORRECT]
   - B) Email servers automatically block all emails containing the word "jewelry."
   - C) Luxury clients only read printed newspapers.
   - D) Most smartphones do not support email apps.

2. **According to luxury retail mobile messaging benchmarks, what is the average reply rate for a personalized, 1-to-1 clienteling text message?**
   - A) Under 5%
   - B) 10% to 15%
   - C) 45% to 60% [CORRECT]
   - D) Exactly 100%

3. **What are the four components of the High-Conversion Luxury SMS Anatomy?**
   - A) Discount code, expiration date, website URL, unsubscribe link.
   - B) Personal Greeting, Specific Personal Anchor, Styled Visual Media, Low-Friction Question. [CORRECT]
   - C) Store hours, driving directions, parking validation, manager phone number.
   - D) Gemological certificate number, wholesale cost, carat weight, store policy.

4. **Why should sales associates avoid opening an outreach message with "I'm just checking in"?**
   - A) It is grammatically incorrect in luxury retail.
   - B) It signals that the associate has no meaningful value to offer and is merely fishing for a sale. [CORRECT]
   - C) It is a trademarked slogan owned by major hotel chains.
   - D) Telephone carriers block messages with the word "checking."

5. **Which of the following represents an effective, high-open luxury email subject line?**
   - A) "BIG WEEKEND SALE: 20% OFF ALL DIAMONDS!"
   - B) "The emerald we discussed on Tuesday + a quick video for you, Sarah" [CORRECT]
   - C) "Jewelry Newsletter Volume 14"
   - D) "URGENT: Don't miss out on this deal!"

6. **What is a "low-friction question" at the conclusion of an outreach message?**
   - A) A question that requires a simple, conversational response rather than a commitment to buy (e.g. "Do you prefer the round or cushion profile?"). [CORRECT]
   - B) Asking the client for their credit card number immediately.
   - C) Asking if they are ready to sign a purchase contract today.
   - D) Inquiring about their annual household income.

7. **What lighting condition is best when photographing jewelry on a smartphone for client outreach?**
   - A) Direct, harsh sunlight at high noon.
   - B) Complete darkness using the phone's built-in flash.
   - C) Diffused, indirect natural daylight or balanced 5500K salon lighting that shows authentic body color without glare. [CORRECT]
   - D) Fluorescent yellow workshop bulbs.

8. **Why is video outreach (a 10-second video of jewelry moving under light) more persuasive than a static photograph?**
   - A) Videos automatically download to the client's desktop computer.
   - B) It dynamically captures light return, fire, scintillation, and true three-dimensional proportions. [CORRECT]
   - C) Luxury clients only buy jewelry featured in full-length movies.
   - D) Video files cannot be forwarded to competitors.

9. **What ink and paper standard should be maintained for handwritten client notes?**
   - A) Lined notebook paper written with red ballpoint pen.
   - B) Heavy, watermarked cotton stationery penned in archival blue or black fountain/gel ink. [CORRECT]
   - C) Standard sticky notes attached to printed receipts.
   - D) Highlighting printed store brochures with fluorescent markers.

10. **If a client does not reply to your first personalized text message, what is the best protocol?**
    - A) Send the exact same text message three times daily until they respond.
    - B) Call them repeatedly from different phone numbers.
    - C) Wait 3 to 4 weeks, then reach out with a completely different, fresh angle (such as a complimentary cleaning invitation or workshop event). [CORRECT]
    - D) Delete their profile from the CRM permanently.
