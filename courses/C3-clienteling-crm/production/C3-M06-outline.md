# C3-M06 Course Outline: VIP Tiering and Time Allocation

## 1. Metadata
- **Course:** C3 Clienteling & CRM: Building a Client Book That Compounds
- **Module:** 6
- **Title:** VIP Tiering and Time Allocation
- **Track:** Foundations
- **Target Personas:** P1 (New Associate), P2 (Maison Client Advisor), P3 (Cruise/Travel Specialist)
- **Estimated Study Time:** 45–60 minutes
- **Core KPIs:** VIP Client Retention Rate, Share of Wallet per Tier, Personal Time Utilization Efficiency

## 2. Learning Objectives
1. Apply the 80/20 luxury Pareto principle to segment client portfolios into actionable tiers.
2. Define the spend thresholds, service privileges, and contact cadences for Tier 1 (VIC), Tier 2 (Core), and Tier 3 (Emerging).
3. Protect dedicated daily clienteling time blocks (the 45-minute morning power hour) against showroom distractions.
4. Execute private salon appointments and bespoke VIP trunk show activations that drive five-figure tickets.

## 3. Structural Breakdown
- **Section 1:** The Luxury Pareto Law (Why equal time distribution fails).
- **Section 2:** The 3-Tier Luxury Portfolio Architecture.
- **Section 3:** Time Blocking: The Daily Associate Operating System.
- **Section 4:** High-Touch VIP Experiences: Salons, Trunks, and Atelier Previews.
- **Section 5:** Managing UHNW Expectations: Discretion, Speed, and White-Glove Boundaries.
- **Section 6:** Actionable Floor Drills & Weekly Practice Protocol.
