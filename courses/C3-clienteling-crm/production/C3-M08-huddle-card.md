# C3-M08 Morning Huddle Card: Measuring Retention and Repeat Business

## Core Principle
"You cannot manage what you do not measure. If you only track your total gross sales, you are driving a car by looking in the rearview mirror. Track your clienteling leading indicators to control your future."

## Key Focus Points (5-Minute Team Sync)
1. **Track Leading Indicators:** Focus on Data Capture Rate (85%+), 2-2-2 Task Completion (95%+), and Weekly Appointments (3–5 per week). The gross revenue will follow automatically.
2. **The 3-Year Vision:** In Year 1, you build the foundation. In Year 2, the book begins to re-engage. In Year 3, your repeat clientele carries 75% of your quota, freeing you from walk-in anxiety.
3. **Execute the Monthly Audit:** On the last Friday of every month, spend 45 minutes auditing your client book growth and celebrate your top relationship milestones!

## Daily Floor Challenge
Calculate your personal Repeat Client Revenue % for the past 60 days. Set an ambitious goal to increase it by 10% over the next quarter!
