# C3-M03 Morning Huddle Card: The 2-2-2 Cadence Explained

## Core Principle
"If you only contact a client when you want to sell them something, you are not clienteling, you are bothering them. Contact them to celebrate them, care for them, and protect their jewelry."

## Key Focus Points (5-Minute Team Sync)
1. **Day 2 is PURE GRATITUDE:** Never pitch a product in the 2-day note. A handwritten note celebrating their milestone and thanking them sets the foundation for life.
2. **Week 2 is CUSTODIAL CARE:** Check on fit and comfort: "How did the necklace wear at Saturday's dinner? Let's bring it in next week so our bench jeweler can inspect the clasp."
3. **Month 2 is THE CURATED HORIZON:** Present the natural next chapter: "Since you loved the yellow sapphire earrings, a matching bezel pendant just arrived from our atelier that I held for you."

## Daily Floor Challenge
Review all sales from 2 days ago, 2 weeks ago, and 2 months ago. Execute every pending 2-2-2 task before 11:00 AM today!
