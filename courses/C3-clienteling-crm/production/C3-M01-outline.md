# C3-M01 Course Outline: Why a Client Book Is Your Career Asset

## 1. Metadata
- **Course:** C3 Clienteling & CRM: Building a Client Book That Compounds
- **Module:** 1
- **Title:** Why a Client Book Is Your Career Asset
- **Track:** Foundations
- **Target Personas:** P1 (New Sales Associate), P2 (Maison Client Advisor), P3 (Cruise/Travel Retail Specialist)
- **Estimated Study Time:** 45–60 minutes
- **Core KPIs:** Personal Sales Volume, Repeat Client Revenue %, Client Lifetime Value (LTV), Appointment Volume

## 2. Learning Objectives
1. Contrast transactional clerking with relationship-driven advisory selling in luxury fine jewelry.
2. Calculate the compounding mathematics of Client Lifetime Value (LTV) across a 5-year relationship lifecycle.
3. Understand the concept of "career equity" and personal client books as portable professional reputation within trade legal/ethical boundaries.
4. Establish daily clienteling disciplines that insulate sales professionals from showroom traffic fluctuations.

## 3. Structural Breakdown
- **Section 1:** The Walk-In Dependency Trap (The 1st-of-the-month reset).
- **Section 2:** The Compounding Mathematics of Client Lifetime Value (LTV Models).
- **Section 3:** The Evolution of the "Black Book" (Index cards to Cloud CRMs).
- **Section 4:** Legal, Ethical, and Professional Boundaries (NDAs, Trade Secrets, and Personal Goodwill).
- **Section 5:** The Daily Habit: Building Relational Equity Before the Doors Open.
- **Section 6:** Actionable Floor Drills & Weekly Implementation Plan.
