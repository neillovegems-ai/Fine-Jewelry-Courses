## Terminal Outcomes

The learner can:
- Articulate the compounding career mathematics of Client Lifetime Value (LTV) versus transactional selling.
- Capture comprehensive client profiling data at point-of-sale without friction or customer discomfort.
- Execute the systematic 2-2-2 post-purchase follow-up cadence (2 days / 2 weeks / 2 months).
- Maintain proactive occasion calendars for anniversaries, birthdays, and multi-year jewelry upgrades.
- Write high-response personalized outreach across SMS/WhatsApp, luxury email, and bespoke handwritten stationery.
- Segment client portfolios into actionable VIP tiers (VIC, Core, Emerging) and protect dedicated daily clienteling time blocks.
- Maintain impeccable CRM data hygiene (objective notes, standardized tagging taxonomy, zero-overdue task pipelines, data privacy compliance).
- Self-audit client book retention metrics, repeat purchase velocity, and annual client spend growth.

**Assessment:** 30-day client-book audit (capture rate, 2-2-2 follow-up completion, appointment booking velocity).  
**KPI link:** Repeat client revenue %, appointment volume, average annual client spend, customer retention rate.  
**Production Media Manifest:** [C3-media-manifest.md](production/C3-media-manifest.md) (40 live, verified figures & frameworks).

## Production Rules & Six-Step SOP

Each module is constructed per the project's rigorous six-step standard:
1. **Outline & Pedagogical Blueprint** (`production/C3-Mxx-outline.md`)
2. **Comprehensive Long-Form Article** (`articles/Mxx-*.md`) with no-cap word depth (~3,500–5,000 words), luxury scenarios, and verbatim dialogue.
3. **Step 2 Self-Critique & Rubric** (Scored across outcome alignment, factual discipline, floor readiness, voice, scannability, persona fit).
4. **Fact-Check Table** (`production/C3-Mxx-factcheck.md`) documenting all claims, citations, and risk classifications.
5. **Repurposing Artifacts** (`production/C3-Mxx-huddle-card.md` and `production/C3-Mxx-quiz.md` with 10 questions).
6. **Option B Visual Enrichment** (5 figures/frameworks per module with responsive luxury card styling, published to WordPress and Tutor LMS).
