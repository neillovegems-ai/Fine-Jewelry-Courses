## Terminal Outcomes

The learner can:
- Execute strict two-person opening and closing procedures with dynamic rotational visual all-clear signals.
- Enforce the one-item-out showcase rule, wrist keyholder retention, and counter custody geometry.
- Deconstruct and neutralize organized multi-confederate distraction theft teams and sleight-of-hand stone switching using the 5-point 10x loupe inspection protocol.
- Navigate federal anti-money laundering (AML) cash reporting under IRS/FinCEN Form 8300 rules ($10,000 threshold, 24-hour and 12-month aggregation, electronic BSA E-Filing, annual customer statements, and strict anti-tipping-off compliance).
- Protect the boutique against non-cash payment fraud (EMV chip liability shifts, cashier's check cleared-funds hold periods, remote telephone order interception, and Fedwire settlement verification).
- Execute the 3-tier inventory auditing hierarchy (twice-daily case piece counts, weekly blind cycle audits, quarterly bench sweeps, and 7-year retained annual physical inventories).
- Follow life-safety armed robbery survival doctrines (absolute non-resistance, verbal narration, discreet sensory observation, post-incident 15-minute emergency timeline, and forensic crime scene preservation).

**Assessment:** Jewelers Mutual Safety & Security Academy completion plus a 30-day store security SOP audit.  
**KPI link:** Shrinkage % (<0.2%), zero preventable losses, 100% Form 8300 compliance, zero payment chargeback write-offs.  
**Production Media Manifest:** [C4-media-manifest.md](production/C4-media-manifest.md) (40 live, verified figures & operational frameworks).

## Production Rules & Six-Step SOP

Each module is constructed per the project's rigorous six-step standard:
1. **Outline & Pedagogical Blueprint** (`production/C4-Mxx-outline.md`)
2. **Comprehensive Long-Form Article** (`articles/Mxx-*.md`) with no-cap word depth (~3,500–5,000 words), luxury scenarios, and verbatim dialogue.
3. **Step 2 Self-Critique & Rubric** (Scored across outcome alignment, factual discipline, floor readiness, voice, scannability, persona fit).
4. **Fact-Check Table** (`production/C4-Mxx-factcheck.md`) documenting all claims, citations, and risk classifications.
5. **Repurposing Artifacts** (`production/C4-Mxx-huddle-card.md` and `production/C4-Mxx-quiz.md` with 10 questions).
6. **Option B Visual Enrichment** (5 figures/frameworks per module with responsive luxury card styling, published to WordPress and Tutor LMS).
