# Course C4: Module 2 — Knowledge Check Quiz
## Opening and Closing: The Two-Person Rule

### 10-Question Comprehensive Assessment

**1. Why do opening and closing times represent the highest statistical risk window for violent robbery in retail fine jewelry?**
- A) Security guards are legally required to take mandatory meal breaks at those times
- B) Electronic alarm systems are completely disarmed, safes are open or being accessed, and public pedestrian traffic is minimal [CORRECT]
- C) Insurance policies do not cover losses that occur before 10:00 AM
- D) Computer servers are performing automatic nightly data backups
*Rationale: During opening and closing, physical perimeter alarms are turned off, heavy safes are unlocked, and the showroom lacks public witnesses, creating ideal conditions for an ambush.*

**2. What is the mandatory role of the second employee (the Observer) during a two-person opening procedure?**
- A) Hold the front door open for the first employee while carrying the daily bank deposit
- B) Walk immediately behind the first employee to assist with searching dark rooms
- C) Remain inside a locked vehicle at a safe observation distance with a phone ready to call 911 [CORRECT]
- D) Go to a local coffee shop and wait for a text message
*Rationale: If both employees enter together, an ambush attacker captures both simultaneously. The second employee must stay outside in a locked vehicle ready to summon emergency assistance.*

**3. What is an "All-Clear Signal" in luxury retail jewelry operations?**
- A) A verbal shout across the showroom that the store is open
- B) A specific, inconspicuous physical marker set by the first employee to visually signal to the observer outside that the premises are secure [CORRECT]
- C) A green LED light installed on the building exterior by the municipal police department
- D) An automated email sent to corporate headquarters
*Rationale: An all-clear signal is an inconspicuous physical visual cue (a specific book position, blind slat angle, or display card) that proves the internal sweep is complete and no intruder is holding the employee.*

**4. What must Employee B (the Observer) do if Employee A enters the building, but after three minutes, NO all-clear signal appears?**
- A) Knock on the front door and shout Employee A's name
- B) Enter using their own key to check if Employee A forgot the signal
- C) Call Employee A on their cell phone and ask why the signal isn't set
- D) Drive away immediately to a safe location and call 911 to report an armed robbery/ambush [CORRECT]
- E) Wait another 15 minutes before taking action
*Rationale: The absence of an all-clear signal must always be treated as an active ambush. Entering the store or lingering outside places the observer in lethal jeopardy and eliminates the outside lifeline.*

**5. Why must the physical object or location of the all-clear signal rotate on a regular schedule?**
- A) To satisfy municipal commercial window display aesthetics
- B) To prevent external criminals who are casing the boutique from learning the signal and forcing Employee A to display it during an ambush [CORRECT]
- C) To comply with OSHA ergonomic guidelines
- D) Because display merchandise rotates weekly
*Rationale: If criminals casing the store identify a static signal (e.g., a specific coffee mug in the window), they will force the opening employee to put it in place under gunpoint.*

**6. What is a "duress code" on a commercial electronic alarm keypad?**
- A) A master code that permanently erases all alarm logging data
- B) A specialized numerical code that appears to disarm the alarm system normally but secretly transmits an immediate panic/hostage signal to the central station [CORRECT]
- C) The code used by emergency medical technicians during a cardiac arrest
- D) A password required to adjust showroom thermostat settings
*Rationale: A duress code disarms the local sirens to satisfy an armed captor, but immediately dispatches police for an armed robbery in progress with a hostage.*

**7. When the boutique reaches official closing time, but three customers are still completing custom orders, what is the mandatory door protocol?**
- A) Leave the exterior doors unlocked until all customers have departed
- B) Immediately lock the entrance doors; assign one associate to monitor the door, unlocking it only to escort exiting clients out one at a time [CORRECT]
- C) Turn off all showroom lights to signal that the customers must leave
- D) Begin removing diamond showcases while clients are watching to speed up the process
*Rationale: Locking doors at closing prevents new, opportunistic individuals from entering during the vulnerable closing window while existing clients finish their transactions.*

**8. What does "dual custody" mean regarding safe and vault access?**
- A) The store manager and assistant manager share a single set of safe keys
- B) Two distinct security credentials (e.g., two different combination halves, or a key and a digital PIN) held by two separate employees are required to open the safe [CORRECT]
- C) The vault must be inspected twice a year by an independent locksmith
- D) Merchandise must be counted by two associates simultaneously during annual inventory
*Rationale: Dual custody guarantees that no single individual can open the safe alone, eliminating internal theft risks and protecting employees from solo extortion or home kidnapping.*

**9. When transferring high-value jewelry trays from showroom cases into the overnight vault at closing, what is the required operational sequence?**
- A) Gather all trays from the entire showroom onto a single rolling cart and leave it by the vault door
- B) Move trays case by case; lock each display showcase immediately after clearing it, and never begin safe put-away until all exterior doors are locked and all clients have left [CORRECT]
- C) Put away the safe items first while clients are still browsing in the front
- D) Leave diamond solitaires in the display cases overnight if the curtains are closed
*Rationale: Cases must be cleared systematically only after doors are locked and customers have left. Concentrating all inventory on an open rolling cart creates a catastrophic target.*

**10. How should closing employees depart the store at the end of the night?**
- A) One employee leaves early while the other finishes locking up alone
- B) Both employees set the perimeter alarm together, exit together, lock the exterior door, visually scan the parking area, and observe each other safely start their vehicles before driving away [CORRECT]
- C) Employees should leave through different exits without speaking
- D) Employees should wait inside their parked cars for 30 minutes to ensure no alarms trigger
*Rationale: Simultaneous departure ensures mutual protection. Observing each other start and drive away eliminates the risk of an employee being ambushed in the parking lot.*
