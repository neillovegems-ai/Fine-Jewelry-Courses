# Course C4: Module 5: Knowledge Check Quiz
## Cash Over the Threshold: Form 8300 Step by Step

### 10-Question Comprehensive Assessment

**1. What is the statutory monetary threshold that triggers the mandatory filing of IRS/FinCEN Form 8300?**
- A) Exactly $5,000 in cash
- B) More than $10,000 in cash received in a single transaction or related transactions [CORRECT]
- C) Any transaction exceeding $50,000 regardless of payment method
- D) Exactly $2,500 for foreign visitors
*Rationale: Form 8300 is legally required whenever a trade or business receives more than $10,000 in cash in one transaction or two or more related transactions.*

**2. Which of the following payment instruments is considered "cash" under IRS Form 8300 rules when purchasing fine jewelry?**
- A) A personal check drawn on the customer's personal bank account
- B) A bank wire transfer initiated from a domestic bank
- C) A cashier's check or bank draft with a face value of $10,000 or less received in a consumer durable goods sale [CORRECT]
- D) A payment processed via an American Express corporate credit card
*Rationale: Under 31 CFR 1010.330, cashier's checks, money orders, and bank drafts with face amounts of $10,000 or less are treated as cash in retail sales of jewelry.*

**3. Within how many calendar days must Form 8300 be filed following the receipt of reportable cash?**
- A) 3 business days
- B) 15 calendar days [CORRECT]
- C) 30 calendar days
- D) By the end of the fiscal quarter
*Rationale: IRS regulations mandate that Form 8300 must be filed within 15 calendar days after the date the reportable cash payment was received.*

**4. Effective January 1, 2024, how must businesses that file 10 or more information returns submit Form 8300?**
- A) By mailing physical paper copies via certified US Postal Service mail
- B) By transmitting scanned PDF copies to an IRS fax number
- C) Electronically using the FinCEN BSA E-Filing System [CORRECT]
- D) By handing physical copies to a local IRS field agent
*Rationale: As of January 1, 2024, the IRS requires electronic filing of Form 8300 through the BSA E-Filing System for businesses filing 10+ information returns.*

**5. What document must the business furnish to each customer named on a Form 8300, and by what annual deadline?**
- A) A full photocopy of the completed Form 8300 by December 31
- B) A written notification statement stating the business name, total cash reported, and IRS filing notice on or before January 31 of the following year [CORRECT]
- C) A certificate of tax exemption by April 15
- D) A formal thank-you letter within 24 hours of purchase
*Rationale: Businesses must furnish a written disclosure statement (such as an annotated sales receipt) to the customer on or before January 31 of the year following the transaction.*

**6. If the business files a Form 8300 and checks the "Suspicious Transaction" box, what is the mandatory rule regarding the customer notification statement?**
- A) The business must immediately send a certified letter warning the customer
- B) The business must NOT send the annual written statement to the customer, and must never inform the customer of the filing [CORRECT]
- C) The customer must be invited to the store to sign a confirmation release
- D) The statement must be delivered in person by the store manager
*Rationale: IRS guidelines state: 'If the suspicious transaction box is checked, do not send form as the statement. Under no circumstances should the parties on the form be made aware of a suspicious filing.'*

**7. What is "structuring" in the context of federal anti-money laundering (AML) laws?**
- A) Organizing display cases by gemstone hardness and karat purity
- B) Deliberately breaking down a cash payment into amounts under $10,000 to evade Form 8300 reporting requirements [CORRECT]
- C) Setting up an LLC to purchase wholesale diamond inventory
- D) Dividing a sales commission between two sales associates
*Rationale: Structuring (or smurfing) is the illegal practice of splitting transactions (e.g., paying $9,000 today and $9,000 tomorrow) specifically to circumvent federal reporting.*

**8. What should an associate do if a client says: "Can I pay $9,500 in cash now and $9,500 tomorrow so your store doesn't have to file that federal cash report?"**
- A) Agree to the request to deliver flexible customer service
- B) Politely inform the client that federal law requires aggregating related cash transactions and prohibits structuring, and document the incident [CORRECT]
- C) Tell the customer to pay the second installment in gift cards
- D) Charge the customer an additional 5% convenience fee
*Rationale: Associates must explain that splitting payments does not bypass reporting under the 12-month related-transactions rule, and structuring is a federal crime.*

**9. Under the 12-month related-transactions rule, which of the following scenarios triggers Form 8300?**
- A) A customer buys a $6,000 watch with cash in January, and returns in March to pay $5,000 in cash for a diamond band as part of an anniversary suite [CORRECT]
- B) A customer buys a $12,000 ring with a personal bank check
- C) A customer charges $25,000 on a Visa credit card
- D) A customer initiates a $50,000 bank wire transfer
*Rationale: Related transactions occurring within a 12-month period that aggregate to more than $10,000 in cash trigger Form 8300 upon receiving the payment that crosses the threshold.*

**10. What identification must an associate verify and record when a customer completes a reportable cash transaction?**
- A) A gym membership card and a utility bill
- B) Government-issued photo ID (Driver's License or Passport) and Taxpayer Identification Number (SSN or ITIN) [CORRECT]
- C) Two social media account profiles
- D) A verbal statement of employment
*Rationale: Form 8300 requires verified taxpayer identification (SSN/ITIN or passport for foreign nationals) and government-issued photo ID.*
