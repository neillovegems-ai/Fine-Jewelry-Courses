# Daily Huddle Card: Course C4: Module 8
## During and After an Incident: Armed Robbery Protocols

### ⏱️ 5-Minute Morning Briefing for the Sales Floor

**Core Principle:** *Merchandise is replaceable. You are not. During a robbery, comply 100%, keep your hands visible, and do exactly what you are told.*

---

### 1. The Survival Rules During an Armed Robbery
- **DO NOT RESIST:** Give them whatever they demand. Diamonds are insured; your life is priceless.
- **Hands Up, Palms Open:** Keep your hands visible at shoulder level. Never drop hands below counter level.
- **Speak Before Moving:** Never make a sudden motion. Tell the assailant what you are doing before you move: *"I am opening the bottom safe drawer now."*
- **Look at the Shoes and Neck:** Don't stare into their eyes. Glance at their shoes, height against the door frame, neck tattoos, and scars.

---

### 2. The First 15 Minutes After the Robbery
1. **Lock the Door Immediately:** Turn the deadbolt the instant they leave. Never walk outside to watch them drive away.
2. **Call 911:** Request police and medical assistance immediately.
3. **Touch Nothing:** Do not pick up dropped jewelry, do not wipe glass, do not touch door handles.
4. **Separate and Write:** Every employee sits at a separate desk with a notepad. Write down what you saw **before speaking to each other**.
