### Pedagogical Objectives
1. **The Psychology of Counter Custody:** Deconstruct how luxury customer service and vigilant asset protection operate as an integrated discipline. Demonstrating meticulous care for a diamond signals its immense value and authenticity to the buyer while signaling uncompromising vigilance to potential thieves.
2. **The Cardinal Showcase Disciplines:**
   - The **One-Item-Out Rule** (strict physical containment; showing one high-value piece at a time; two items permitted only for direct side-by-side comparison on a dedicated presentation tray, returning the first piece immediately).
   - **Showcase Key Custody:** The absolute mandate of the wrist coil keyholder (keys never left in the lock cylinder, never placed on the glass counter, never hung on under-counter hooks).
   - **Case Relocking Protocol:** Closing and engaging plunger locks immediately every time an item is retrieved.
3. **Counter Topography & Spatial Geometry:**
   - The 45-degree consultative stance (avoiding squared-off vulnerability; maintaining physical balance).
   - Show tray positioning (placing the velvet tray within associate arm's reach, maintaining physical custody between the associate's hands and the client).
   - Creating a spatial barrier against sudden reach-and-grab attempts.
4. **The Anti-Switch 10x Loupe Protocol:**
   - The twin inspection ritual (louping the diamond in front of the client *before* handing it over, and louping it immediately upon receiving it back).
   - Verifying the 5 diagnostic markers: GIA laser inscription, characteristic natural inclusion mapping, prong integrity, metal assay hallmarks, and weight/feel.
   - Reframing the inspection as white-glove connoisseurship rather than client suspicion.
5. **Private Salon Escort & High-Ticket Protocol:**
   - Threshold value triggers (goods exceeding $25k–$50k moved to enclosed private salons).
   - Escort sequences and door custody.
   - Requiring government-issued photo ID prior to presenting six-figure loose stones.
