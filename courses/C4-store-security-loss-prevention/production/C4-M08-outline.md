## Module 8: During and After an Incident: Armed Robbery Protocols (Pedagogical Blueprint)

- **Track:** Core Foundations &bull; Operations & Compliance Track

### Pedagogical Objectives
1. **The Psychology of an Armed Encounter:** Explain the physiological and psychological impact of armed robbery (acute adrenaline surge, tunnel vision, auditory exclusion, time distortion). Establish the paramount, non-negotiable guiding principle: **Life Safety Trumps All Property**. Fine jewelry can be remade, recut, and reimbursed by insurance; human life cannot.
2. **Behavioral Protocols During an Armed Robbery:**
   - The Cardinal Rule: **Absolute Non-Resistance**. Never argue, fight, reach for a concealed weapon, or feign heroism.
   - Hands in Plain Sight: Keep both hands elevated at chest level or resting flat on the counter. Never make sudden movements or reach into pockets.
   - Verbal Communication: Speak in a calm, clear tone. Always verbally narrate your physical actions before moving: *"I am going to reach into my left pocket to retrieve the safe key."*
   - Avoid Direct Eye Staring: Do not stare aggressively into the assailant's eyes (which attackers interpret as defiance or mental recording); observe sensory markers discreetly.
3. **Discreet Sensory Observation:**
   - How to observe identifying characteristics without provoking the assailant: Focus on physical features that cannot be easily altered (height against in-store door height strips, skin tone, eye color, neck/hand tattoos, scars, vocal accents, and shoes).
4. **The Post-Incident 15-Minute Protocol:**
   - Step 1: Immediate Lockdown (Lock the entrance doors immediately after assailants depart; protect remaining staff; prevent intruders from returning).
   - Step 2: Emergency 911 Dispatch (Provide real-time tactical facts: weapon type, direction of flight, vehicle description, injuries requiring EMS).
   - Step 3: Forensic Crime Scene Preservation (Touch nothing; rope off counters; preserve shell casings, dropped tools, and fingerprints).
   - Step 4: Independent Witness Separation (Prevent collective memory distortion; each associate writes their own independent description sheet).
   - Step 5: Regulatory & Insurance Notification (Contact corporate leadership, Jewelers Mutual Claims, and the Jewelers' Security Alliance).
