# Daily Huddle Card: Course C4 — Module 6
## Cards, Checks, Wires and Remote-Order Fraud

### ⏱️ 5-Minute Morning Briefing for the Sales Floor

**Core Principle:** *A receipt is not payment. Payment is settled funds in your bank account. Never let an urgent customer rush you past verification.*

---

### 1. The EMV Chip Rule (Zero Exceptions)
- Never key-enter a credit card manually on the terminal if the customer is physically standing in front of you.
- Never allow a customer to swipe a chip card. If the chip reader errors out three times, **do not override it with a swipe**. Request another card or a bank wire.
- **Why?** If you swipe a chip card and it is stolen, the bank takes 100% of the money back via chargeback, and insurance will NOT pay a dime.

---

### 2. The Check Policy: "Cleared Funds Only"
- When a client pays with a personal check or cashier's check:
  - Do NOT release the jewelry the same day.
  - Do NOT assume calling the bank to "verify funds" is enough.
  - Explain politely: *"Under our standard store policy, custom pieces and high-value jewelry are released upon final bank clearance, which takes 3 to 5 business days."*

---

### 3. Remote Order Red Flags: The "Overnight Rush"
If someone calls or emails from three states away wanting to buy a $15,000 diamond ring and pay with a credit card:
- **Red Flag 1:** They never ask about diamond cut or quality, only if you can ship it today.
- **Red Flag 2:** They demand overnight priority shipping to an address that does not match the card billing address.
- **Action:** Require a direct bank wire for first-time out-of-state buyers. Honest clients will understand; fraudsters will immediately disappear.
