# Course C4: Store Security, Loss Prevention & High-Value Transaction Protocols
## Module 2: Opening and Closing: The Two-Person Rule (Pedagogical Blueprint)

- **Track:** Core Foundations &bull; Operations & Compliance Track
- **Personas:** P1 (New Sales Associate), P3 (Cruise/Travel Specialist), P7 (Store Leader / Boutique Director)
- **Word Target:** ~3,500–4,500 words (No-cap policy)
- **Research Anchor:** Reference R7 & R8 (Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Career-and-Skills-Reference.md Section 3.1–3.3); Fine-Jewelry-Career-Guide.md (Dual-custody opening/closing workflows); Jewelers Mutual Safety & Security Academy (Opening and Closing Procedures); Jewelers' Security Alliance (Ambush and Kidnapping Guidelines).

### Pedagogical Objectives
1. **The Vulnerability Window:** Explain why opening and closing procedures represent the highest-risk hours of the retail jewelry operating day (alarms disabled, safes open, minimal staffing, absence of public foot traffic).
2. **The Two-Person Opening Protocol:** Detail the exact multi-stage sequence of the mandatory Two-Person Rule:
   - Arrival and external perimeter survey (drive-by check for suspicious vehicles, loiterers, forced doors, ladders).
   - The Observer/Scout dynamic (First Person enters; Second Person remains in vehicle at safe observation distance with vehicle locked, engine running, and phone ready to dial 911).
   - Internal sweep and alarm deactivation (under-the-timer code entry, checking restrooms, offices, drop-ceilings, back exits).
   - Setting the All-Clear signal.
3. **The All-Clear Signal System:** Design and implement dynamic, tamper-evident visual all-clear markers:
   - Placement rules (visible from the exterior parking area but inconspicuous to passersby).
   - Daily rotational protocols (changing marker positions or items to prevent habituation).
   - The Negative Rule: The absence of the signal or a displaced signal is an active ambush call.
4. **Closing Rituals & Safe Storage:** Deconstruct the end-of-day lockdown:
   - Locking exterior doors promptly at closing time while customers finish purchases.
   - Dual-custody safe combination and time-lock protocols (no single employee possesses all combinations/keys).
   - Case-by-case tray transfers to the safe.
   - Final internal search before arming perimeter and motion sensors.
   - Two-person simultaneous departure.
