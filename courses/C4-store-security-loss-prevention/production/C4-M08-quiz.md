# Course C4: Module 8 — Knowledge Check Quiz
## During and After an Incident: Armed Robbery Protocols

### 10-Question Comprehensive Assessment

**1. What is the paramount, non-negotiable operational priority during an armed robbery in a fine jewelry boutique?**
- A) Protecting the store's highest-carat diamond inventory
- B) Life safety and personal survival of all staff and clients [CORRECT]
- C) Activating the fire sprinkler system to disperse smoke
- D) Recording the event on a smartphone camera for social media
*Rationale: Life safety supersedes all physical property. Fine jewelry is fully insured and replaceable; human life cannot be replaced.*

**2. How does an associate's physical resistance affect the statistical outcome of an armed robbery?**
- A) Resistance increases the probability of capturing the criminal by 90%
- B) Resistance dramatically increases the probability of severe physical injury or death by over 80% [CORRECT]
- C) Resistance guarantees that the insurance deductible is waived
- D) Resistance causes the assailants to surrender immediately
*Rationale: FBI and JSA statistics confirm that resisting armed assailants escalates lethal violence, drastically increasing the likelihood of fatal gunshots or assaults.*

**3. Where should an associate keep their hands throughout an armed robbery?**
- A) Tucked inside their coat pockets to stay warm
- B) Raised to shoulder level with palms open and clearly visible to the assailants at all times [CORRECT]
- C) Under the counter resting near the alarm button
- D) Behind their back with wrists crossed
*Rationale: Visible hands prevent an anxious, high-strung assailant from suspecting that the associate is reaching for a concealed weapon or alarm trigger.*

**4. If an armed assailant orders an associate to open a showcase and the key is in their pocket, how should the associate execute the movement?**
- A) Reach rapidly into the pocket to show physical agility
- B) Verbally state their intention clearly before moving: *"The key is in my left pocket. I am reaching down slowly now to get it."* [CORRECT]
- C) Refuse to move until police arrive
- D) Drop to the floor and pretend to faint
*Rationale: Assailants are under extreme adrenaline. Any sudden, unannounced movement toward a pocket can trigger an immediate, lethal reflex shot.*

**5. How should an associate observe an armed assailant without provoking aggressive retaliation?**
- A) Stare aggressively directly into the robber's eyes
- B) Avoid direct, confrontational eye contact; focus discreetly on unchangeable physical features like height against door markers, tattoos, scars, and footwear [CORRECT]
- C) Ask the robber to remove their mask for a moment
- D) Point their finger at the robber's face while speaking
*Rationale: Staring into an attacker's eyes is perceived as defiance or aggressive memorization; glancing at shoes, posture, and physical markings gathers critical intelligence safely.*

**6. What is the very first physical action employees must take the moment the robbers exit the store?**
- A) Run out to the sidewalk to shout for assistance
- B) Immediately lock and deadbolt the entrance doors to secure the building [CORRECT]
- C) Clean up broken glass to prevent customer tripping hazards
- D) Post an update on the store's website
*Rationale: Locking the door immediately prevents suspects from returning, secures the premises against secondary opportunists, and protects the crime scene.*

**7. Why is it critical that employees DO NOT touch broken glass, counters, or discarded tools after a robbery?**
- A) Glass shards can damage company vacuum cleaners
- B) Touching surfaces destroys critical forensic evidence, including latent fingerprints, palm prints, and touch DNA [CORRECT]
- C) Insurance policies require a professional cleaning contractor to sweep
- D) Broken glass must remain intact for 30 days under federal law
*Rationale: Crime scene preservation allows forensic police technicians to lift fingerprints, tool marks, and DNA evidence left by the perpetrators.*

**8. Why must witnesses be separated to write down their suspect descriptions independently before conferring with each other?**
- A) To prevent employees from arguing about who made the biggest sale
- B) To eliminate cognitive memory contamination, ensuring each witness records an authentic, untainted account [CORRECT]
- C) Because state labor laws require solitary reflection after trauma
- D) To allow the manager to select the best writer to file the police report
*Rationale: Discussing an event collectively contaminates memory; one confident associate can unintentionally alter the recollections of other witnesses.*

**9. What clothing item is considered one of the most reliable visual identifiers of a fleeing robbery suspect?**
- A) Hats, because they are impossible to remove
- B) Footwear/shoes, because suspects frequently discard jackets, hoodies, and masks while fleeing, but rarely change shoes [CORRECT]
- C) Sunglasses, because criminals keep them on permanently
- D) Gloves, because they have unique serial numbers
*Rationale: Fleeing suspects frequently shed coats, masks, and caps in getaway vehicles or alleyways, but almost never change their sneakers or work boots.*

**10. In addition to local police and emergency medical services, which industry security organization should be notified immediately following a robbery?**
- A) The International Olympic Committee
- B) The Jewelers' Security Alliance (JSA) [CORRECT]
- C) The Federal Trade Commission
- D) The Gemological Institute of America grading laboratory
*Rationale: JSA analyzes crime data in real time, coordinating with regional police departments and issuing flash alerts to neighboring jewelers across the state.*
