### Pedagogical Objectives
1. **The Mathematics of Retail Shrink:** Define shrinkage in fine jewelry (unexplained inventory loss across internal theft, external theft, administrative errors, and vendor discrepancies). Contrast the general retail average (~1.5% of sales) with the luxury jewelry benchmark (target <0.2%–0.3% shrink). Explain why a single missing 2-carat diamond wipes out thousands of dollars in net operating margin.
2. **The 3-Tier Inventory Verification System:**
   - *Tier 1: Daily Morning & Evening Case Counts:* Fast, non-negotiable piece count per showcase. Verifying that the number of physical pieces in Tray A equals the tray slot capacity or log count before opening and before locking the vault.
   - *Tier 2: Weekly Blind Cycle Counts:* Rotating category audits (e.g., Week 1: Bridal diamond rings; Week 2: High-end watches; Week 3: Loose stones; Week 4: Gold bracelets). The "Blind" principle: Counter staff receive a list of locations, NOT item quantities.
   - *Tier 3: Annual Comprehensive Physical Inventory:* Complete barcode scanning, weighing, and appraisal reconciliation retained off-premises for 7+ years per Jewelers Mutual guidelines.
3. **The Discrepancy Investigation Tree:**
   - The critical 24-hour resolution window: Why investigating a missing piece within 24 hours yields an 85%+ recovery rate, while waiting 30 days reduces recovery to under 10%.
   - The 5-point investigative sequence:
     1. POS Sales Log audit (was it sold today but not marked off?).
     2. Repair & Custom Intake bench review (is it in a shop envelope?).
     3. Memo Out / Vendor Return check (is it out on approval?).
     4. Safe & Under-Counter Sweep (did it slip behind a velvet tray?).
     5. Synchronized CCTV Video Audit (reviewing camera logs for the specific case).
4. **Quarterly Bench Sweeps & Memo Controls:**
   - Bench sweeps: Sweeping jeweler benches, polishing wheels, ultrasonic cleaning tanks, and scrap sweeps to recover unmounted stones and metal dust.
   - Memo tracking: Enforcing strict 30-day limits on consignments and memo goods.
5. **Insurer Compliance Calendar:**
   - Quarterly/semi-annual inventory updates to insurance brokers reflecting current gold spot prices.
   - Annual January broker export and photo archive retention.
