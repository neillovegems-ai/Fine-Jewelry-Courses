# Course C4: Module 6: Knowledge Check Quiz
## Cards, Checks, Wires and Remote-Order Fraud

### 10-Question Comprehensive Assessment

**1. How does the EMV liability shift rule affect a jewelry boutique when an in-store transaction is processed using a counterfeit card?**
- A) The card-issuing bank automatically absorbs 100% of the financial loss
- B) If the merchant fails to use an EMV chip terminal and instead swipes the magnetic stripe, 100% of the fraud liability shifts directly to the merchant [CORRECT]
- C) The municipal police department reimburses the retailer from forfeiture funds
- D) The transaction is covered under standard homeowner's insurance
*Rationale: Under EMV rules, whichever party has the lesser technology (e.g., merchant swiping a chip card) absorbs 100% of counterfeit fraud losses.*

**2. Does a standard Jewelers Mutual commercial insurance policy cover financial losses resulting from credit card chargebacks?**
- A) Yes, all chargebacks are covered up to policy limits
- B) No, chargeback losses and non-EMV liability shifts are explicitly excluded from coverage [CORRECT]
- C) Only if the cardholder was a foreign national
- D) Yes, but only for transactions under $5,000
*Rationale: Insurers consider payment card fraud and chargebacks a commercial credit risk, not physical casualty loss; insurance policies explicitly exclude chargeback losses.*

**3. Why is it dangerous to release high-value fine jewelry immediately after calling a bank to confirm that a cashier's check has "available funds"?**
- A) Bank phone operators frequently charge high consultation fees
- B) A confederate can withdraw the funds right after the phone call, or the check may be counterfeit, stolen, or subject to a stop-payment order [CORRECT]
- C) Cashier's checks are legally classified as unsecured promissary notes
- D) Calling a bank violates federal consumer privacy regulations
*Rationale: Verbal verification only confirms that money was in an account at that second; it does not settle funds or protect against fraudulent check drafts.*

**4. What is the only safe and definitive operational rule for releasing merchandise paid for by personal or cashier's check?**
- A) Wait until the customer provides two references
- B) Wait until the check has officially cleared and final settlement has been credited to the boutique's bank account [CORRECT]
- C) Release the merchandise immediately if the customer leaves their business card
- D) Have the customer sign a personal promissory note
*Rationale: The only foolproof protection against bad checks is waiting for full clearing and bank settlement before relinquishing physical possession.*

**5. What is the most suspicious red flag in an unsolicited telephone or email inquiry for high-value diamonds?**
- A) The buyer asks detailed questions about GIA grading certificates
- B) The buyer requests a formal written appraisal
- C) The buyer does not negotiate price, demands immediate overnight shipment, and supplies a shipping address different from the billing address [CORRECT]
- D) The buyer asks to schedule an in-person viewing appointment next week
*Rationale: Fraudsters care only about obtaining the merchandise quickly before the stolen card is reported; they never negotiate price and demand immediate remote shipment.*

**6. What does Address Verification Service (AVS) evaluate during an electronic credit card transaction?**
- A) The customer's credit score rating
- B) Whether the numerical street address and postal code provided by the customer match the cardholder's billing records at the issuing bank [CORRECT]
- C) The physical distance between the store and the customer's residence
- D) The customer's social security registration status
*Rationale: AVS compares the numerical street number and 5-digit ZIP code entered at POS against the issuing bank's database to detect unauthorized card use.*

**7. Why is a bank wire transfer considered the gold standard for high-ticket remote jewelry sales exceeding $25,000?**
- A) Bank wires can be reversed at any time by the buyer without explanation
- B) Bank wires are processed through the Federal Reserve Fedwire system and represent cleared, irrevocable funds once settled into the merchant's account [CORRECT]
- C) Bank wires are completely anonymous and exempt from government oversight
- D) Bank wires carry zero transaction processing fees
*Rationale: Fedwire funds transfers are irrevocable once completed and confirmed by the receiving bank, completely eliminating chargeback and counterfeit check risks.*

**8. What is a common tactic used by credit card fraudsters after placing a remote phone order with an authorized billing address?**
- A) Calling back after the package is shipped to divert or reroute delivery to a secondary pickup location or hotel [CORRECT]
- B) Requesting that the package be sent via standard ground delivery
- C) Asking the boutique to include a physical gift receipt
- D) Demanding that the package be held for 30 days before shipping
*Rationale: Fraudsters supply the real cardholder's billing address to pass AVS screening, then intercept the package by calling the courier to redirect delivery to a trap address.*

**9. When verifying a customer's physical credit card during an in-store counter purchase, what physical security check should an associate perform?**
- A) Rub the magnetic stripe with a magnet to test polarity
- B) Verify that the name on the card matches the customer's government photo ID, and inspect the card for signs of re-embossing, shaving, or altered hologram stickers [CORRECT]
- C) Check if the expiration date falls on a leap year
- D) Verify that the card was issued within the same state
*Rationale: Altered or counterfeit physical cards often show re-embossed numbers, shaved raised lettering, or damaged signature strips.*

**10. What is the fundamental difference between an ACH electronic transfer and a Federal Reserve wire transfer?**
- A) ACH transfers are faster than wire transfers
- B) An ACH transfer can be reversed or recalled by the sender's bank for up to 60 days in cases of unauthorized debit claims, whereas a wire transfer is final and non-recallable upon settlement [CORRECT]
- C) ACH transfers only operate during weekend hours
- D) Wire transfers can only be executed in foreign currencies
*Rationale: ACH payments are subject to NACHA return rules and chargeback claims up to 60 days; wire transfers provide immediate, irrevocable settlement.*
