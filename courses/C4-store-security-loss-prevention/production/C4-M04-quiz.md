# Course C4: Module 4: Knowledge Check Quiz
## Distraction Theft, Switches and Grab-and-Run

### 10-Question Comprehensive Assessment

**1. What is the fundamental psychological exploit that distraction theft rings use against retail sales associates?**
- A) They use violent physical intimidation to compel compliance
- B) They exploit the associate's eagerness to please, retail greed, and susceptibility to sensory confusion [CORRECT]
- C) They forge municipal building inspection certificates
- D) They hack into the store's point-of-sale Wi-Fi network
*Rationale: Distraction thieves exploit hospitality and commercial eagerness, creating controlled chaos so the associate willingly leaves cases unlocked or presents multiple items.*

**2. In a professional 4-person distraction theft team, what is the specific role of the "Disrupter"?**
- A) To cut the main electrical power cables outside the building
- B) To create sensory commotion, demand minor services, or create a mess to divert employee attention away from the showcases [CORRECT]
- C) To appraise the stolen diamonds in the parking lot
- D) To forge fake GIA certificates
*Rationale: The Disrupter creates sudden noise, spills a beverage, asks complex off-topic questions, or demands quick watch battery changes to pull associates away from active cases.*

**3. What is the primary physical countermeasure that completely neutralizes a diamond switch theft during a sales consultation?**
- A) Asking the customer to sign an affidavit of honesty before trying on the ring
- B) Examining the diamond with a 10x loupe immediately before hand-off AND immediately upon receiving it back [CORRECT]
- C) Only showing diamonds that are under 0.50 carats
- D) Keeping an armed guard standing behind the customer's chair
*Rationale: Examining the piece under a 10x loupe upon return catches the counterfeit substitute instantly while the suspect is still at the counter, preventing the switch.*

**4. Where should customer presentation mirrors be located on the showroom floor to prevent grab-and-run thefts?**
- A) Directly beside the front entrance door for maximum natural light
- B) Outside on the sidewalk in front of the boutique windows
- C) Mounted on interior structural walls located deep within the showroom, away from exit avenues [CORRECT]
- D) On the ceiling directly above the entrance threshold
*Rationale: Placing mirrors deep inside the showroom forces the customer to walk away from exit doors, eliminating the short sprint needed for a grab-and-run.*

**5. If an opportunistic customer grabs a $30,000 diamond solitaire from the counter and sprints out the front door, what is the employee's mandatory response?**
- A) Sprint after the suspect down the street to tackle them and recover the merchandise
- B) Remain inside the store, lock the entrance doors immediately, ensure all staff are safe, and dial 911 [CORRECT]
- C) Throw a showcase chair through the front window to alert pedestrians
- D) Post the customer's description on social media before contacting police
*Rationale: Jewelers Mutual and JSA strictly prohibit chasing suspects. Chasing leads to fatal shootings, knife attacks, or vehicle hit-and-runs by awaiting getaway cars.*

**6. Why do distraction thieves frequently bring small children or baby strollers into a luxury fine jewelry showroom?**
- A) To qualify for corporate family discount programs
- B) Strollers provide concealed storage compartments for sweeping entire display trays, while children create natural noise and distraction [CORRECT]
- C) To comply with state retail childcare regulations
- D) Because children are allowed to handle loose diamonds legally
*Rationale: Strollers provide voluminous blind spots and deep compartments where stolen trays can be concealed in seconds, while crying children create intense sensory distraction.*

**7. How should a boutique team respond when a group of five unfamiliar visitors enters simultaneously and scatters across different showcase counters?**
- A) Allow each person to browse unattended while the solo associate focuses on the best-dressed individual
- B) Immediately speak the internal store code word, bringing all available colleagues to the sales floor to assign one associate per visitor [CORRECT]
- C) Turn off the showroom lighting to encourage them to leave
- D) Ask the visitors to wait outside on the sidewalk
*Rationale: A group scattering across the showroom is a classic distraction tactic. The team must deploy "one-on-one" coverage immediately, ensuring no visitor is near an open case.*

**8. What physical characteristic of a synthetic moissanite or CZ stone can an associate identify under a 10x loupe to detect a diamond switch?**
- A) Moissanite displays strong optical doubling (double refraction), while diamond is singly refractive; CZ lacks natural clarity inclusions and laser inscriptions [CORRECT]
- B) Moissanite dissolves instantly when touched with a microfiber cloth
- C) CZ reflects only yellow light regardless of cut grade
- D) Diamonds always display visible blue magnetic radiation under a loupe
*Rationale: Moissanite's extreme birefringence (0.043) produces obvious doubling of facet junctions under a loupe, whereas natural diamonds show crisp, single facet edges and natural inclusions.*

**9. What should an associate do if a customer asks to view a $15,000 diamond pendant outdoors in "real sunlight"?**
- A) Walk out to the sidewalk with the customer while holding the piece in hand
- B) Hand the piece to the customer and let them step outside alone
- C) Politely decline, explaining that company insurance mandates all viewings occur inside, and escort them to a standardized daylight viewing booth [CORRECT]
- D) Allow it only if the customer leaves their house keys on the counter
*Rationale: Merchandise must never be taken outside the secure physical perimeter of the store. Color assessment in sunlight must be performed in a calibrated daylight viewing box.*

**10. Why is "cash flashing" (pulling out a thick roll of $100 bills immediately upon entry) considered a classic distraction theft red flag?**
- A) Federal law prohibits transactions using physical paper cash
- B) It is deliberate psychological bait designed to induce euphoria and greed, blinding the associate to standard security protocols [CORRECT]
- C) Paper currency is known to damage delicate jewelry showcases
- D) It indicates the customer is an undercover IRS auditor
*Rationale: Professional criminals know that flashing large cash rolls triggers excitement in sales associates, leading them to drop their guard and violate basic custody rules.*
