# Daily Huddle Card: Course C4 — Module 3
## Showing High-Value Goods: Floor Discipline

### ⏱️ 5-Minute Morning Briefing for the Sales Floor

**Core Principle:** *White-glove luxury presentation IS loss prevention. The exact steps that demonstrate reverence for high jewelry make theft physically impossible.*

---

### 1. The Three Non-Negotiable Showcase Rules
1. **Keys on Your Wrist, Never in the Lock:** Your showcase key must be secured to a wrist coil or lanyard attached to your person at all times. If you let go of the key, it retracts to your wrist. Never leave a key sitting in a glass sliding door cylinder.
2. **Close and Lock Immediately:** Slide the door shut and engage the plunger lock the instant your hand clears the case. Never turn around to present an item while the showcase behind you remains open.
3. **The One-Item Rule:** Only one high-value piece out at a time. If the client asks to compare two rings, you may bring out a second ring on a velvet show tray—**never three**. Before bringing out a third, one must return to the locked case.

---

### 2. The Loupe Ritual (The Anti-Switch Defense)
- **Before Hand-Off:** Pick up your 10x triplet loupe. Examine the stone in front of the client. Say:
  > *"Allow me to verify the prong tension and check the laser inscription for you before you try this on."*
- **After Return:** When the client hands the ring back, **do not put it straight into the showcase**. Hold it, wipe it with your polishing cloth, loupe it, and confirm the girdle inscription or inclusion footprint.
- **Why It Works:** Professional switchers rely on speed. If they see you loupe the piece upon return, they know an instant switch will be caught immediately.

---

### 3. Show Tray Geometry
- Always present pieces on a weighted velvet show tray positioned on your side of the counter centerline.
- Maintain your hands resting lightly at the edge of the tray. Never push an open tray across the counter within easy reach of a seated visitor while your attention is diverted.
