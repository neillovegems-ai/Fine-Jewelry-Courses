# Daily Huddle Card: Course C4 — Module 1
## Why Jewelry Is Targeted: The Threat Landscape

### ⏱️ 5-Minute Morning Briefing for the Sales Floor

**Core Principle:** *Fine jewelry is the most concentrated, liquid, and untraceable portable wealth on earth. Criminals do not see art or romance—they see instant cash with low recovery odds.*

---

### 1. The Three Red Flags of Pre-Operational Casing
Before an attack or distraction theft occurs, crews almost always conduct pre-operational reconnaissance. Watch for:
- **The "Floor Scanner" Stance:** A visitor whose eyes track ceiling corners, cameras, and display case hinges rather than jewelry pieces.
- **The "Shift Noter":** A visitor who asks subtle logistical questions: *"Do you have a jeweler on site today?"*, *"How many people usually work the evening shift?"*, or *"Does that front door lock automatically?"*
- **The "Quick Turnover" Phone User:** A visitor who remains on an active phone call or audio earbud, pacing the perimeter while subtly pointing their device toward display cases.

---

### 2. The 60-Second Reality of Smash-and-Grab
- Smash-and-grab crews rely on speed and shock. They target display cases nearest to entry/exit doors.
- **Countermeasure:** Never concentrate all high-ticket inventory (large solitaire diamonds, luxury Swiss watches) in a single showcase near the front entrance. Distribute high-value merchandise deeper into the store salon.

---

### 3. Immediate Team Action Drills
1. **Greet Every Person at the Threshold:** Eye contact and a direct verbal greeting within 5 seconds destroys the criminal advantage of anonymity.
2. **Never Show High-Value Goods with Back to the Door:** Position yourself so you maintain clear sightlines to the primary showroom entrance.
3. **If You Sense Casing:** Use the store's discreet alert code to notify management and security immediately.
