# Daily Huddle Card: Course C4 — Module 2
## Opening and Closing: The Two-Person Rule

### ⏱️ 5-Minute Morning Briefing for the Sales Floor

**Core Principle:** *Never enter or exit a jewelry showroom alone. The moments when alarms are disarmed and safes are open are the most dangerous 30 minutes of your day.*

---

### 1. The Opening Sequence (Memorize Your Role)
- **Employee A (The Scout):** Approaches the store, inspects the front door, glass, and exterior perimeter. Enters alone, locks door behind them, disarms alarm within designated grace period, and conducts a complete walk-through of the showroom, restrooms, vault area, and back offices.
- **Employee B (The Observer):** Remains inside a locked vehicle parked across the lot with a direct line of sight to the all-clear window. Engine running, mobile phone in hand with 911 pre-keyed. Never looks at social media or leaves the vehicle.

---

### 2. The All-Clear Signal Rules
- Once Employee A verifies the store is 100% secure, they display the agreed **All-Clear Signal** (e.g., placing the green retail display stand on the third shelf, or turning the window blind wand to 45°).
- **The Cardinal Rule:** If Employee B sees **NO signal**, a **WRONG signal**, or sees Employee A waving from the window, Employee B **DOES NOT ENTER**. Employee B drives away immediately and calls 911: *"I am observing an ambush robbery in progress at [Store Name]."*

---

### 3. The Closing Lockdown
1. **Lock the Front Door at Closing Time Sharp:** Even if clients are still browsing inside, lock the exterior doors. Station an associate at the door to let exiting clients out one by one.
2. **Two People Stay Until the Vault is Sealed:** Never begin putting away display trays into the safe until the showroom is completely clear of customers and doors are deadbolted.
3. **Walk Out Together:** Both closing employees exit together, visually scan the parking lot, and watch each other safely reach and start their vehicles.
