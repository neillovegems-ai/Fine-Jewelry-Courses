# Course C4: Module 7 — Knowledge Check Quiz
## Cycle Counts, Case Counts and Shrink

### 10-Question Comprehensive Assessment

**1. What is the standard annual shrinkage benchmark targeted by premier fine jewelry retailers, compared to general department stores?**
- A) Fine jewelry targets 5.0% shrink due to high foot traffic
- B) Fine jewelry targets under 0.2% to 0.3% shrink, compared to 1.4%–1.6% in general retail [CORRECT]
- C) Fine jewelry has zero shrinkage because insurance reimburses all losses
- D) Exactly 10% across all luxury categories
*Rationale: Fine jewelry deals in concentrated high-ticket inventory; shrinkage exceeding 0.3% severely impacts net operating profitability.*

**2. What is the fundamental operational principle of a "blind" cycle count?**
- A) The inventory auditor wears dark tinted glasses while scanning tags
- B) The counter staff is provided with SKU numbers and locations, but is NOT provided with the system's expected quantity on hand [CORRECT]
- C) Only damaged or returned goods are counted
- D) The count is performed in the dark using ultraviolet blacklights
*Rationale: Providing expected quantities leads to confirmation bias where counters assume missing items are an error; blind counts force exact physical verification.*

**3. Why must morning and evening showcase piece counts be performed every single operating day?**
- A) To satisfy municipal fire department occupancy regulations
- B) To detect any theft, loss, or misplacement within a 12-hour window before associates leave the store and trails go cold [CORRECT]
- C) To calculate the hourly electricity consumption of showcase LED spotlights
- D) To determine which associate earns the highest daily bonus
*Rationale: Daily case counts ensure discrepancies are caught on the same day they occur, when sales transactions, staff memories, and CCTV footage are immediate.*

**4. For how many years does Jewelers Mutual require fine jewelry retailers to retain documented, itemized physical inventory records off-premises?**
- A) Exactly 1 year
- B) A minimum of 7 years [CORRECT]
- C) 30 days following the annual audit
- D) Indefinitely for 50 years
*Rationale: Jewelers Mutual underwriting standards require comprehensive itemized inventory records, invoices, and photos to be retained off-site for at least 7 years.*

**5. When an associate notices an empty slot in an engagement ring tray during evening closing, what is the first operational step in the discrepancy tree?**
- A) Call the municipal police department to report an armed robbery
- B) Check the daily POS sales log to verify if the piece was legitimately sold during the shift [CORRECT]
- C) Accuse the newest employee of internal theft
- D) Write off the item in the accounting software immediately
*Rationale: The most common cause of an apparent discrepancy is an administrative lag where an item was legitimately sold but not immediately deducted from the visual tray log.*

**6. What is a "quarterly bench sweep" in a fine jewelry retail operation that maintains an in-house custom or repair shop?**
- A) Sweeping the floors of the retail showroom with an electronic metal detector
- B) A systematic audit of the jeweler's bench, repair envelopes, scrap containers, and polishing filters to reconcile unmounted stones and precious metal sweeps [CORRECT]
- C) Replacing all workbench tools every 90 days
- D) Cleaning customer jewelry for free every quarter
*Rationale: Bench sweeps account for loose stones undergoing setting/repair, unissued memo gems, and gold/platinum sweeps sent to refiners.*

**7. Why should high-end diamond and watch inventory lists provided to commercial insurance brokers be updated quarterly or semi-annually?**
- A) To prevent the store's computer systems from running out of memory
- B) To ensure policy coverage limits accurately reflect fluctuations in commodity gold spot prices and current wholesale diamond markets [CORRECT]
- C) Because insurance policies expire every 90 days
- D) To adjust the store's retail sales tax rate
*Rationale: Rapid increases in gold or diamond prices can leave a store severely under-insured if coverage limits are based on outdated historical inventory costs.*

**8. What should happen to the showroom exit doors while staff is investigating an unresolved evening showcase count discrepancy?**
- A) Doors should be propped open so staff can search the sidewalk
- B) Exterior doors remain locked and deadbolted; no staff member leaves the premises until the 5-point trace is completed [CORRECT]
- C) Customers are invited back in to assist with searching
- D) Doors are unlocked to allow cleaning crews early entry
*Rationale: Maintaining perimeter lockdown prevents accidental loss or unauthorized removal of merchandise while the discrepancy is actively investigated.*

**9. What is the role of time-stamped CCTV video audit in the 5-point discrepancy trace?**
- A) To monitor which associate takes the longest lunch break
- B) To visually review the exact moment the showcase was unlocked, identifying every person who interacted with that specific tray [CORRECT]
- C) To broadcast live showroom video to the store's social media channels
- D) To measure ambient customer foot-traffic patterns
*Rationale: Reviewing targeted CCTV footage provides an objective visual record of every transaction, showing whether the piece was placed on a tray, dropped, or stolen.*

**10. Why is maintaining strict 30-day memo tracking essential to loss prevention?**
- A) Consignment and memo goods represent outside vendor capital that must be returned, paid for, or re-certified to prevent untracked inventory drift [CORRECT]
- B) Memo goods lose 50% of their carat weight if kept in a safe for more than 30 days
- C) State law prohibits jewelers from holding consignment goods overnight
- D) Memo stones are not eligible for GIA grading
*Rationale: Untracked memo items create severe administrative confusion, obscuring true shrinkage and creating severe legal liability with vendor partners.*
