# Course C4: Module 1 — Knowledge Check Quiz
## Why Jewelry Is Targeted: The Threat Landscape

### 10-Question Comprehensive Assessment

**1. Why is fine jewelry uniquely targeted by professional organized crime rings compared to other high-ticket consumer goods?**
- A) Jewelry carries higher insurance policy payouts for retailers
- B) Extreme value-to-weight ratio, immediate market liquidity, and ease of dismounting/melting into untraceable components [CORRECT]
- C) Jewelry store employees are legally prohibited from carrying defensive tools
- D) Jewelry prices are fixed internationally by government commodity boards
*Rationale: Fine jewelry represents unmatched portable wealth. Diamonds can be dismounted, laser inscriptions polished off, and gold melted within hours, erasing provenance.*

**2. According to Jewelers' Security Alliance (JSA) data, what is the typical duration of a commercial smash-and-grab retail raid?**
- A) 10 to 15 minutes
- B) 3 to 5 minutes
- C) 60 to 90 seconds [CORRECT]
- D) Exactly 15 seconds
*Rationale: Smash-and-grab crews move in coordinated blitzes, using sledgehammers or heavy mallets to breach cases and sweep merchandise into bags within 60 to 90 seconds before police can dispatch.*

**3. What is the primary operational objective of a multi-person distraction theft team?**
- A) To physically intimidate the store manager into opening the vault
- B) To split sales floor attention, create sensory overload, and leave showcase displays open and unattended [CORRECT]
- C) To disable the store's primary electrical circuit breaker
- D) To force an associate to accept counterfeit cashier's checks
*Rationale: Distraction teams use confederates to monopolize staff attention, stage mild commotions, or demand complex merchandise viewings, allowing an accomplice to sweep an unlocked showcase.*

**4. When a prospective customer enters a showroom and immediately displays a thick roll of $10,000 in cash, how does the JSA categorize this behavior?**
- A) A premier high-net-worth VIP buying signal requiring immediate private salon escort
- B) A standard legitimate payment method common in high-ticket luxury jewelry
- C) A recognized behavioral red flag designed to induce greed and distract the associate from security protocols [CORRECT]
- D) A mandatory trigger to offer a 10% cash discount
*Rationale: Legitimate luxury clients rarely flash large rolls of physical cash to sales associates upon entry. JSA identifies cash-flashing as classic baiting used to blind associates to distraction theft.*

**5. How does Underwriters Laboratories (UL 972) burglary-resistant laminate protect showcase integrity during a physical assault?**
- A) It reflects 99% of ambient lighting to blind the attacker
- B) It prevents the glass from breaking entirely under any circumstance
- C) It bonds glass fragments to high-tensile polymer layers, resisting penetration for several minutes and defeating rapid smash-and-grab tactics [CORRECT]
- D) It automatically triggers a silent police alarm upon physical vibration
*Rationale: Burglary-resistant laminate doesn't prevent glass cracking, but holds fractured layers together under repeated heavy strikes, denying rapid access within the attackers' critical 60-90 second window.*

**6. Which physical layout feature represents a severe operational vulnerability in a luxury jewelry boutique?**
- A) Locating the most expensive diamond solitaires and Swiss watches in showcases adjacent to the front entrance [CORRECT]
- B) Installing dual-custody time locks on the primary overnight safe
- C) Utilizing velvet show trays with recessed presentation wells
- D) Having 100% video camera coverage across all display counters
*Rationale: Placing high-value showcases near the front door provides smash-and-grab and grab-and-run attackers with an immediate escape route, reducing their exposure time to seconds.*

**7. What is the primary purpose of greeting every visitor immediately upon crossing the showroom threshold?**
- A) To assess their apparel and determine their creditworthiness
- B) To eliminate criminal anonymity, establish direct visual contact, and demonstrate active sales floor alertness [CORRECT]
- C) To immediately ask for their government photo ID before allowing browsing
- D) To comply with municipal retail trade licensing regulations
*Rationale: A prompt, professional greeting establishes that the visitor has been seen and noted, removing the perceived cloak of anonymity that opportunistic criminals rely upon.*

**8. What behavior during a browsing visit strongly suggests pre-operational reconnaissance (casing)?**
- A) Asking detailed questions about diamond color saturation and cut grades
- B) Comparing a GIA grading report against an AGS diamond certificate
- C) Focusing attention on camera placements, ceiling sensor positions, and showcase locking mechanisms rather than the jewelry [CORRECT]
- D) Requesting to see a piece under both diffused and direct LED spotlights
*Rationale: Legitimate buyers scrutinize gemstones and craftsmanship; individuals casing a store monitor physical security infrastructure, staffing numbers, and blind spots.*

**9. What does the term "traveling criminal ring" mean in the context of retail jewelry asset protection?**
- A) Itinerant antique dealers trading uncertified estate pieces between gem shows
- B) Highly organized, mobile syndicates that move across states or countries, hitting multiple jewelry stores using specialized distraction or burglary tactics before local law enforcement can coordinate [CORRECT]
- C) Sales representatives carrying vendor memo lines across regional retail territories
- D) Foreign luxury tourists purchasing duty-free jewelry in port destinations
*Rationale: Traveling crime rings operate on a regional or international scale, hitting dozens of stores with identical distraction or burglary playbooks and leaving the jurisdiction immediately.*

**10. How should an associate respond if they notice subtle indicators that a group of visitors is attempting to set up a distraction theft scenario?**
- A) Confront the visitors directly and accuse them of being distraction thieves
- B) Immediately lock all open showcases, keep all merchandise within physical control, and alert colleagues using the internal coded phrase [CORRECT]
- C) Run out of the showroom to locate a police officer
- D) Offer the visitors free beverages to buy time
*Rationale: The associate must secure all merchandise immediately, maintain physical custody of the counter, and alert team members using established discreet protocols without creating panic.*
