# Course C4: Module 3 — Knowledge Check Quiz
## Showing High-Value Goods: Floor Discipline

### 10-Question Comprehensive Assessment

**1. What is the operational definition of the "One-Item-Out Rule" in luxury fine jewelry retailing?**
- A) A sales associate may only sell one piece of jewelry to each customer per visit
- B) Only one piece of high-value merchandise is removed from the showcase and shown to a customer at any given time [CORRECT]
- C) Only one customer is allowed inside the retail showroom at a time
- D) An associate is only allowed to hold one showcase key in their possession
*Rationale: The one-item-out rule limits physical exposure to a single piece, preventing distraction thieves from confusing the associate or sweeping multiple items.*

**2. Where should showcase keys be kept throughout an associate's shift on the sales floor?**
- A) Inserted in the showcase lock cylinder for quick access during busy periods
- B) Placed neatly on top of the counter glass beside the presentation tray
- C) Attached directly to the associate's person via a secure wrist coil or retractable lanyard [CORRECT]
- D) Kept inside an unlocked drawer beneath the cash register
*Rationale: Keys must remain physically attached to the associate's wrist or body at all times, preventing them from being left in the lock or swiped by a distraction accomplice.*

**3. What should an associate do with the showcase door immediately after removing a requested diamond engagement ring?**
- A) Leave the door open six inches in case the client wants to see another ring immediately
- B) Close the sliding door and immediately engage the lock before turning to present the piece [CORRECT]
- C) Ask a neighboring associate to stand in front of the open door
- D) Prop the door open with a presentation cloth
*Rationale: Leaving showcase doors unlocked or ajar while presenting merchandise is the primary vulnerability exploited by distraction theft teams.*

**4. Why is it mandatory to examine a diamond with a 10x loupe both BEFORE handing it to a client and IMMEDIATELY AFTER receiving it back?**
- A) To clean fingerprints off the table facet for aesthetic purposes
- B) To verify the diamond's authenticity, laser inscription, and physical inclusions, defeating sleight-of-hand stone switching [CORRECT]
- C) To check if the ambient store lighting is operating at 5500K color temperature
- D) To calculate the state sales tax applicable to the diamond's cut grade
*Rationale: The pre-and-post loupe examination verifies that the exact diamond shown is the exact diamond returned, preventing counterfeit CZ or synthetic moissanite switches.*

**5. How should an associate professionally frame the anti-switch loupe inspection to an honest customer so it does not feel insulting?**
- A) State bluntly: *"Company policy requires me to verify you didn't steal our diamond."*
- B) Frame it as white-glove quality assurance: *"Allow me to check the prong seating and verify the laser inscription for you before you try this piece on."* [CORRECT]
- C) Perform the inspection secretly behind a wall in the backroom
- D) Skip the inspection if the customer appears wealthy and well-dressed
*Rationale: Framing the inspection as high-end custodial care and verification of diamond security elevates the luxury experience while maintaining rigid loss prevention.*

**6. If a customer is deciding between two distinct diamond solitaires, how should the associate manage the presentation?**
- A) Place both rings on a velvet show tray within associate custody; before showing a third, return one to the locked case [CORRECT]
- B) Bring out four additional rings so the customer has a complete spectrum to evaluate
- C) Allow the customer to hold one ring in each hand while walking to the entrance mirror
- D) Hand the customer the entire showcase inventory tray to inspect at their leisure
*Rationale: A maximum of two pieces may be out for direct comparison on a secure tray; introducing a third item requires returning one piece to the locked showcase first.*

**7. Where should the velvet show tray be positioned during a counter consultation?**
- A) Pushed completely across the counter against the client's hands
- B) Held resting on the associate's lap below counter height
- C) On the counter surface on the associate's side of the centerline, with the associate maintaining active hand custody [CORRECT]
- D) On top of the front window display ledge
*Rationale: Positioning the tray on the associate's side of the counter centerline creates a physical buffer, preventing opportunistic reach-and-grab thefts.*

**8. What physical stance should an associate maintain while standing behind the counter presenting fine jewelry?**
- A) A rigid, squared-off posture with elbows locked on the glass
- B) A relaxed 45-degree angle stance that maintains balanced footing, full sightlines of the showroom, and physical mobility [CORRECT]
- C) Sitting on a low stool with feet tucked beneath the counter drawers
- D) Leaning over the counter with both forearms resting forward
*Rationale: A 45-degree stance maintains physical balance and dynamic sightlines across the showroom entrance, preventing the associate from being trapped or knocked off-balance.*

**9. When presenting an exceptional high-jewelry necklace or large loose diamond valued at $75,000+, where should the consultation take place?**
- A) Standing at the front showcase closest to the street entrance
- B) In a dedicated private salon equipped with controlled access and closed doors [CORRECT]
- C) Outside on the sidewalk in direct natural sunlight
- D) At the register counter next to the cash drawer
*Rationale: Ultra-high-value merchandise must be shown in enclosed private viewing rooms, eliminating open-floor exposure and crowd vulnerability.*

**10. What identification credential should be politely requested prior to presenting six-figure loose diamonds or rare colored gemstones in a private salon?**
- A) A personal business card with an embossed company logo
- B) A valid, government-issued photo ID (driver's license or passport) [CORRECT]
- C) A utility bill proving residential address
- D) A social media profile verification badge
*Rationale: Jewelers Mutual and industry security standards recommend verifying and recording government photo ID before presenting exceptionally high-value unmounted diamonds.*
