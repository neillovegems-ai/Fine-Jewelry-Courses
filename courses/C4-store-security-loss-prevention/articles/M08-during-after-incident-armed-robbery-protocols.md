# Module 8: During and After an Incident: Armed Robbery Protocols

The ultimate test of a fine jewelry professional's operational preparation is not how smoothly they conduct an anniversary consultation, nor how accurately they execute a monthly cycle count. The true test occurs during the terrifying seconds when the door bursts open, firearms or heavy weapons are brandished, and the retail salon becomes the scene of a violent, life-threatening robbery.

According to long-term violent crime statistics maintained by the **Jewelers' Security Alliance (JSA)** and the **Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)**, an armed robbery against a retail jewelry store is among the most intense, volatile encounters in commercial retail. The perpetrators operate under extreme physiological adrenaline surges. They are frightened, aggressive, highly unpredictable, and frequently armed with high-capacity semi-automatic firearms, shotguns, or sledgehammers. In this charged environment, a single miscalculated gesture—a sudden reach into a drawer, a defensive argument, a hesitation to obey, or an aggressive stare—can convert a property crime into a tragic homicide.

Decades of operational experience across the international jewelry sector have yielded an absolute, inviolable core doctrine:

> **MERCHANDISE IS 100% REPLACEABLE. HUMAN LIFE IS ENTIRELY IRREPLACEABLE.**

Every diamond solitaire, platinum necklace, and Swiss complication in the boutique is insured by commercial underwriters. No tray of jewelry, regardless of carat weight or monetary value, is worth a human life. 

This capstone module provides retail sales advisors, diamond specialists, and store leaders with the psychological tools, physical survival disciplines, and immediate post-incident protocols required to navigate an armed encounter safely and assist law enforcement in bringing perpetrators to justice.

---

## 1. The Physiological Reality of an Armed Encounter

When an assailant brandishes a weapon and shouts orders, the human body experiences an immediate, massive sympathetic nervous system dump (the "fight-or-flight" response). Heart rates spike instantly above 160 beats per minute, triggering severe perceptual distortions:
- **Tunnel Vision:** The field of vision narrows by up to 70%, focusing obsessively on the weapon's barrel while losing peripheral sight of surrounding associates or accomplices.
- **Auditory Exclusion:** The brain filters out sound, causing shouted commands to seem muffled or silent.
- **Time Distortion:** Events appear to slow down dramatically, making a 60-second encounter feel like thirty minutes.

Understanding these biological responses allows retail professionals to maintain cognitive control. By consciously taking slow, deep diaphragmatic breaths, associates can counteract physiological panic and execute survival protocols with poise.

## 2. The Four Cardinal Survival Disciplines

During an active armed robbery, associates must adhere strictly to four survival rules:

### 1. Absolute Compliance (No Heroics)
- **Do Exactly What You Are Told:** If ordered to open a showcase, open it. If ordered to unlock the safe, unlock it. If ordered to lie flat on the floor, lie flat immediately.
- **Never Argue or Negotiate:** Do not attempt to talk the robbers out of the theft, plead for specific pieces, or offer business advice. 
- **Never Resist or Fight:** Never attempt to wrestle a weapon away, tackle an assailant, or pull a concealed firearm. FBI and JSA statistics confirm that **in jewelry store robberies where employees physically resist, the probability of employee injury or death escalates by more than 80%**. Property is secondary; your sole mission is to ensure that every person in the showroom walks out alive.

### 2. Hands in Plain Sight (Open Palms at Shoulder Height)
To an agitated, trigger-happy assailant, a hand hidden from view is perceived as an armed threat.
- Keep both hands elevated at shoulder height with palms open and fingers spread, clearly visible to the robbers.
- If lying face-down on the floor, extend both arms forward on the carpet with palms flat.
- Never drop a hand below counter level, reach into a coat pocket, or make sudden furtive movements.

### 3. Verbal Narration (Speak Before Moving)
Assailants perceive unexpected physical motion as a defensive attack. If you must move to obey a command, **always verbally announce your physical action before executing it**:
- If ordered to retrieve safe keys from a pocket:
  > *"The safe key is in my right trouser pocket. I am going to reach down slowly with my right hand to take it out. I am not reaching for a weapon."*
- If ordered to open a showcase:
  > *"I am stepping over to the bridal showcase now. The lock is at the bottom right corner."*
Speaking in a calm, respectful, and steady voice de-escalates tension, reduces the assailant's paranoia, and prevents accidental discharges.

### 4. Discreet Sensory Observation (Avoid Staring)
Never stare directly into an armed robber's eyes. In criminal psychology, direct eye contact is interpreted as defiance, disrespect, or an overt attempt to memorize facial features for police lineups, which can provoke violent strikes.
- Look down or avert your direct gaze, using your peripheral vision to observe identifying features that cannot be easily discarded:
  - **Height:** Gauge their height as they pass standardized **Door Height Strips** installed on the entrance frame.
  - **Footwear:** Note their shoes (brand, color, athletic style vs. construction boots). Criminals frequently discard jackets, hoodies, and gloves while fleeing, but rarely change shoes.
  - **Permanent Markers:** Note neck tattoos, teardrop tattoos near the eyes, scars on the hands, or missing teeth.
  - **Voice & Speech:** Listen to speech patterns, specific slang, foreign accents, or names used between confederates.

## 3. The Post-Incident 15-Minute Critical Timeline

The moments immediately following the robbers' exit are critical. Adrenaline is surging, staff members may be in tears or shock, and chaotic confusion threatens to compromise life safety and forensic evidence. 

The store leader or senior associate must immediately take command and execute the **Post-Incident 15-Minute Timeline**:

---

```text
========================================================================
POST-INCIDENT 15-MINUTE TIMELINE CHECKLIST
========================================================================
MINUTE 00 - 02: IMMEDIATE PERIMETER LOCKDOWN
- Lock and deadbolt the exterior showroom doors immediately.
- Prevent suspects from re-entering.
- Under NO CIRCUMSTANCE pursue suspects outside onto the sidewalk.

MINUTE 02 - 05: EMERGENCY SERVICES DISPATCH (911)
- Call 911 immediately.
- State: "We have just experienced an armed robbery at [Store Name, Address]."
- State if anyone is injured and request EMS.
- Provide tactical flight details: number of suspects, weapons, last known 
  direction of flight, vehicle make/model/color/license plate.

MINUTE 05 - 08: FORENSIC CRIME SCENE PRESERVATION
- Touch NOTHING.
- Rope off display cases, counters, and door handles.
- Do not pick up dropped jewelry, tools, or spent casings.
- Keep all employees in a clean, uncontaminated area (e.g., breakroom).

MINUTE 08 - 12: WITNESS SEPARATION & INDEPENDENT LOGS
- Physically separate all associates and clients.
- Provide each person with a blank notepad and pen.
- Every witness writes their own description independently WITHOUT speaking.

MINUTE 12 - 15: CORPORATE, INSURER & JSA NOTIFICATION
- Notify corporate executive leadership.
- Contact Jewelers Mutual Claims Department to open formal loss file.
- Contact Jewelers' Security Alliance (JSA) to initiate regional flash alert.
========================================================================
```

## 4. Crime Scene Integrity & Evidence Preservation

A jewelry store immediately following a robbery is a **Forensic Crime Scene**. Latent physical evidence left by the perpetrators is the single most effective tool detectives possess to identify and arrest the suspects before they can strike another store.

### The Five Rules of Crime Scene Preservation
1. **Touch Absolutely Nothing:** Do not pick up dropped jewelry, display pads, or tools left behind by the robbers (e.g., sledgehammers, pry bars, pillowcases). These objects contain rich deposits of touch DNA and latent ridge fingerprints.
2. **Preserve Door Touchpoints:** Assailants almost always push against door glass or metal push bars when fleeing. Station an associate at the front door to ensure arriving police and paramedics do not touch the exact surface areas contacted by the suspects.
3. **Cordon Off Broken Glass Areas:** Keep all staff away from shattered showcase glass. Sledgehammer strikes frequently generate micro-splinters that cause injuries, and attackers often cut themselves on jagged glass, leaving vital blood DNA.
4. **Export CCTV Video to Encrypted Storage:** The store manager or security officer must immediately isolate and archive the high-definition CCTV video file of the event, exporting an unedited copy with verifiable timestamps to a secure USB drive for detectives.

## 5. Trauma Care, Media Management, and Industry Reporting

Surviving an armed robbery is a severe psychological trauma. The enterprise's responsibility to its team members extends well beyond filing police reports.

### Employee Care & Acute Stress Management
- **Never Force Staff to Reopen Immediately:** The store must remain closed for the remainder of the trading day. 
- **Professional Trauma Counseling:** Leadership should provide immediate access to specialized crisis debriefing counselors (Critical Incident Stress Debriefing - CISD). Recognizing that emotional shock, sleep disturbance, and anxiety are normal human reactions allows staff to heal and recover confidence.

### Media & Social Media Blackout
- Under no circumstances should sales associates post photos, videos, or commentary about the robbery on personal social media channels (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook). 
- All media inquiries from local television or newspapers must be directed exclusively to the boutique's designated corporate spokesperson or legal counsel:
  > *"We are fully cooperating with law enforcement in their active investigation. Our sole focus today is the safety and well-being of our team and clients. All inquiries should be directed to our corporate office."*

### Filing with the Jewelers' Security Alliance
Within hours of the incident, file a comprehensive confidential incident report with the **Jewelers' Security Alliance (JSA)** (www.jewelerssecurity.org). 

JSA maintains an intelligence database that connects crime patterns across all 50 states. By submitting details of the modus operandi, weapon types, and suspect descriptions, your report allows JSA analysts to match the crime against known traveling syndicates, alert neighboring jewelers across the region, and coordinate multi-agency federal investigations.
