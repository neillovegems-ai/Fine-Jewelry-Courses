# C6 Bridal & Engagement Mastery

**Track:** Category Specialization &bull; Bridal & Diamond Counter Track (Track 4)  
**Personas:** P4 (Bridal Specialist), P1 (New Associate), P5 (Custom Design Specialist), P7 (Store Leader / Boutique Director)  
**10 modules, 8–10 learner hours (~42,000 words total)**  
**Research anchor:** GIA (Gemological Institute of America) Diamond Grading System, AGS Laboratories Diamond Cut Proportions, De Beers Historical Retail Case Studies, The Knot 2022–2025 Real Weddings & Engagement Studies, Jewelers of America Bench Standards, FTC Jewelry Guides (16 CFR Part 23), and Jewelers Mutual Insurance Loss Data.

## Terminal Outcomes

The learner can:
- Map the modern bridal buyer's psychological and digital journey, replacing aggressive closing pressure with high-empathy consultative luxury hospitality.
- Transform digital inquiries and walk-in foot traffic into scheduled, curated VIP showroom consultations utilizing pre-appointment mood board parsing and three-tiered tray staging.
- Guide couples through diamond shape, mounting silhouette, and precious metal selection without causing cognitive overload or decision fatigue.
- Conduct an objective, gemologically grounded, and balanced presentation of natural earth-mined diamonds versus laboratory-grown diamonds, transparently addressing secondary market realities and trade-in policies.
- Execute dignified, non-confrontational budget discovery using the Bracket Method, optimizing diamond cut quality over invisible clarity premiums, and positioning financing as empowering cash-flow management.
- Introduce matching wedding bands early during initial silhouette discovery, ensuring flush-fit mechanical compatibility and establishing a 10-year bridal upgrade roadmap.
- Orchestrate joint-couple consultations with diplomatic poise, decoding non-verbal distress signals, utilizing the binary Elimination Bracket, and executing the confidential Wishlist Hand-off protocol.
- Diagnose finger anatomy (prominent knuckles vs conical fingers), enforce realistic 5-week CAD/CAM timelines with mandatory 14-day proposal buffers, and apply pre-delivery 10-point bench QA inspections.
- Support clients through the post-pickup vulnerability window, maintain proposal confidentiality, assist with standalone specialty insurance onboarding, and host in-salon post-proposal champagne celebrations.
- Track and optimize personal business metrics (Consultation Close Rate, Band Attach %, Average Engagement Ticket, Pipeline Velocity, Referral Multiplier) through daily 15-minute morning pipeline audits.

**Assessment:** Comprehensive 100-question curriculum assessment (10 questions per module) and retail floor role-play simulation.  
**KPI link:** Bridal consultation close rate (>60%), wedding band attach rate (>55%), average engagement ticket lift (+25%), referral yield (>0.40x).  
**Production Media Manifest:** [C6-media-manifest.md](production/C6-media-manifest.md) (50 live, verified figures & operational frameworks).

## Production Rules & Six-Step SOP

Each module is constructed per the project's rigorous six-step standard:
1. **Outline & Pedagogical Blueprint** (`production/Mxx-outline.md`)
2. **Comprehensive Long-Form Article** (`articles/Mxx-*.md`) with no-cap word depth (~3,500–5,000 words), gemological specifications, and verbatim dialogues.
3. **Step 2 Self-Critique & Rubric** (`production/Mxx-self-critique-rubric.md`) scored across terminal outcome alignment, factual discipline, floor readiness, voice, and scannability.
4. **Fact-Check Table** (`production/Mxx-fact-check.md`) documenting industry benchmarks (GIA, AGS, The Knot, JA, Jewelers Mutual).
5. **Repurposing Artifacts** (`production/Mxx-daily-floor-huddle.md` and `production/Mxx-quiz.md` with 10 questions).
6. **Option B Visual Enrichment** (5 figures/frameworks per module with responsive luxury card styling, published to WordPress and Tutor LMS).
