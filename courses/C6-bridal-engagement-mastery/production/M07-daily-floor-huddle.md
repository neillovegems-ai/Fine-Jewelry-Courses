# Daily Floor Huddle Card: Module 7 (Two Buyers, One Ring: Couple Dynamics)

### 3-Minute Floor Briefing
- **The 50/50 Eye Contact Rule:** When couples shop together, never talk solely to the bride. Look both partners in the eyes and ask questions that engage both aesthetic dreams and practical value.
- **Watch the Body Language:** If the paying partner leans back or looks away when you mention diamond specs, take the price pressure off immediately. Frame everything as aesthetic discovery.
- **Use the Elimination Bracket:** If a couple can't decide between 5 rings, clear the pad. Show only 2 rings at a time: *"If you walked out the door right now, which one feels more like you?"*
- **Never Take Sides in a Disagreement:** If they debate platinum versus gold, or solitaire versus halo, anchor the choice in daily lifestyle and existing jewelry rather than personal opinion.
- **The Wishlist Hand-off:** Document the bride's dream ring openly, but invite the partner back for a private solo diamond selection to keep the proposal timing and final budget secret.
