# Daily Floor Huddle Card: Module 10 (Bridal Metrics: Tracking Your Own Conversion)

### 3-Minute Floor Briefing
- **The Top 1% Mindset:** Treat your sales desk like your own multi-million dollar business. Track your close rate, band attach rate, and average ticket every week.
- **Plugging Funnel Leaks:** If clients are saying *"we need to think about it"* and vanishing, send a digital wishlist with video within 48 hours to keep the momentum alive.
- **Dollars Over Percentages:** Don't get fooled by a 70% margin on a $2,000 lab diamond. A $10,000 natural diamond at 35% margin brings $3,500 into the store and triples your commission.
- **Revive Stalled Clients:** Look at your pipeline every Monday. Call or text clients who went silent 10 days ago with an exciting new diamond or design update.
- **Execute the 15-Minute Morning Audit:** Spend 15 minutes before opening checking custom orders, sending proposal countdown texts, and preparing your day.
