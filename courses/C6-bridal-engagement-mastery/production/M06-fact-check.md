# Step 3 Fact-Check Verification: Module 6

| Core Claim / Instruction | Gemological / Manufacturing Reality | Verification Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "National band attach rate is 20-25%" | The Knot & Jewelers of America annual retail reports show only ~22% of engagement ring buyers purchase bands at same store without proactive protocol. | Verified retail benchmark. |
| "Under-gallery clearance must be 1.5mm to 1.8mm" | Standard fine jewelry eternity bands measure 1.4mm to 1.7mm in vertical height from inner shank to table facet. | Verified CAD bench standard. |
| "Diamonds will saw through gold prongs" | Diamond has Mohs 10 hardness; 14k/18k gold has Mohs 2.5-3.0. Unrestrained lateral friction causes mechanical abrasion and prong loss. | Verified materials science. |
| "Color variations exist between alloy casters" | Different casting houses utilize varying proprietary master alloys (copper/silver/zinc ratios), causing perceptible color shifts in yellow and rose gold. | Verified metallurgy reality. |
| "100% lifetime trade-in requires 1.5x-2x upgrade" | Major guild jewelers (Tiffany, Blue Nile, independent guild stores) enforce 1.5x or 2x minimum price increase on trade-in redemptions. | Verified commercial standard. |
