# Daily Floor Huddle Card: Module 8 (Sizing, Timelines, Custom and Resizing Realities)

### 3-Minute Floor Briefing
- **The Knuckle Problem:** If a client has a large knuckle and thin finger base, do not size the ring small. Size it to clear the knuckle, and weld sizing beads at 5 and 7 o'clock to stop it from spinning.
- **Account for Band Width:** When sizing for a wide wedding band (4mm-6mm), always use wide ring sizers and add 0.5 size compared to a thin solitaire.
- **The 14-Day Rule:** Never promise custom delivery on the day of the client's proposal. Build in a minimum 14-day safety buffer for re-casting, setting, or sizing adjustments.
- **Eternity Band Warning:** Make sure clients know that full eternity bands cannot be resized. Double-check finger size with precision before ordering.
- **Inspect Before Hand-Off:** Conduct the 10-point QA check on every finished ring. Tap acoustic rattle test, loupe prongs, check silk snagging, and match the GIA laser number.
