# Daily Floor Huddle Card — Module 3: Shape, Setting & Metal

### 3-Minute Morning Briefing for the Floor Team

#### 1. Core Principle of the Day: The 3-Step Funnel
- **NEVER** throw 20 rings at a couple at once!
- Follow the Funnel:
  1. **Step 1:** Pick the Diamond Shape first (Round, Oval, Emerald, Radiant).
  2. **Step 2:** Pick the Setting Architecture (Solitaire, Cathedral, Bezel).
  3. **Step 3:** Pick the Metal (Platinum vs Yellow Gold).

#### 2. The Face-Up Spread Truth
- If a client wants maximum finger coverage for their budget: **Show them an Oval or Pear!**
- A 2.0-carat oval looks ~10% larger on the hand than a 2.0-carat round brilliant because of its elongated surface area!

#### 3. Quick Role-Play Practice Question
- *Customer:* "Why is this cathedral ring better than that simple peg solitaire?"
- *Associate Response:* "Both are beautiful, but notice how the cathedral setting has graceful metal arches sweeping up from the band to support the center diamond. If you accidentally bump your hand against a granite counter or doorframe, the cathedral arches absorb that impact and protect your prongs from bending. It's built to be worn every single day without worry!"
