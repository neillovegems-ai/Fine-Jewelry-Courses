# Daily Floor Huddle Card: Module 1 (The Modern Bridal Buyer's Journey)

### 3-Minute Floor Briefing
- **Validate Online Images First:** When a client shows a screenshot on their phone, never say "we do not sell that" or "that band is too fragile." Praise the aesthetic choice first, then explain how your workshop builds that exact silhouette with proper structural durability.
- **Spot the Buyer Archetype:**
  - *Co-Shoppers:* Speak equally to both partners. Engage the wearer on hand feel and the purchaser on diamond cut and warranty terms.
  - *Hybrid Shoppers:* Validate their partner's taste and assure the purchaser that you will handle the technical execution with care.
  - *Complete Surprise:* Lower the stakes early by explaining your complimentary post-proposal sizing and setting exchange policies.
- **Keep the Pad Clear:** Never clutter the presentation tray with more than three rings at once. Return rejected designs to the vitrine immediately.
- **Step Toward Daylight:** Halogen spotlights flatter every stone artificially. Walk diamonds to a window to demonstrate honest color and fire under natural light.
