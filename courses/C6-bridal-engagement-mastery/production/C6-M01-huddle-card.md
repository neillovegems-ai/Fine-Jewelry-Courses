# Daily Floor Huddle Card — Module 1: The Modern Bridal Journey

### 3-Minute Morning Briefing for the Floor Team

#### 1. Core Principle of the Day: The Pinterest Protocol
- When a client shows you a picture on their phone, **NEVER** say *"We don't sell that"* or *"That band is too thin."*
- **ALWAYS SAY:** *"I love this design! Let’s break down the details—you have an elongated oval with claw prongs and a hidden halo. Let’s look at how our bench crafts this exact look so it lasts a lifetime."*

#### 2. Identify the 3 Buyer Archetypes
1. **Co-Shoppers (Both Here):** Treat both partners as equal decision-makers. Engage both!
2. **Hint-Droppers (Solo with Photos):** Validate the partner's taste and reassure the buyer that you will get the details exactly right.
3. **Pure Surprise (Solo with Zero Clues):** Reassure them with our free ring sizing and exchange policy so they feel 100% safe.

#### 3. Quick Role-Play Practice Question
- *Customer:* "My girlfriend sent me this picture on Instagram, but I have no idea what any of this means."
- *Associate Response:* "She has fantastic taste! What she sent you is an elongated radiant-cut diamond in a delicate four-prong cathedral setting. You are in great hands—I'm going to pull three diamonds from our vault right now so you can see this exact shape in person and feel completely confident. Have a seat, let me grab you an espresso, and let's bring this photo to life!"
