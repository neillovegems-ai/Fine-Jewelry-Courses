# Daily Floor Huddle Card: Module 5 (Budget Conversations That Keep the Romance)

### 3-Minute Floor Briefing
- **Core Floor Principle:** Never blurt *"What is your budget?"* early in an appointment. Explore aesthetics first, then deploy the Bracket Technique: *"We design rings from $3k-$6k, $6k-$10k, and $10k+. Which neighborhood feels most comfortable?"*
- **The Golden Allocation Rule:** Ensure the client understands that the center stone represents ~65% of the total ring cost. Never let a client spend 95% of their budget on a loose diamond only to face sticker shock over the platinum mounting and sales tax.
- **Master the "Invisible Levers":** When a client wants a larger look on a fixed budget, never drop cut quality. Instead, guide them to an eye-clean SI1, a G or H color, or an "under-size" carat mark (e.g., 1.42ct instead of 1.50ct).
- **Dignified Financing:** Introduce 12-month 0% interest programs to all clients as a smart way to keep liquid savings in place for the wedding or honeymoon.
- **Handling Partner Clashes:** If a partner falls in love with a ring over budget, praise the design, record the aesthetic profile, and pivot to curating stone options that deliver that identical silhouette within their comfort zone.
