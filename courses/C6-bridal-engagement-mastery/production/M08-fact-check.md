# Step 3 Fact-Check Verification: Module 8

| Core Claim / Instruction | Gemological / Manufacturing Reality | Verification Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "Wide bands fit tighter than narrow bands" | A wider shank displaces more tissue along the phalanx, creating greater friction and requiring a +0.25 to +0.75 size compensation. | Verified bench mechanics. |
| "Sizing beads prevent top-heavy ring spinning" | Sizing beads (speed bumps) create dual contact points behind the joint, allowing the ring to clear larger knuckles while eliminating rotational play. | Verified goldsmithing standard. |
| "Full eternity diamond bands cannot be resized" | Altering ring circumference requires expanding or contracting metal; with continuous diamond settings, changing diameter breaks prong seats. | Verified jewelry engineering reality. |
| "Thermal expansion affects finger size" | Peripheral vasodilation and constriction cause human finger volume to fluctuate by up to a full size between hot summer and cold winter environments. | Verified physiological metric. |
| "Tension settings cannot be resized post-production" | Tension rings require precise pre-stressed metallurgy (up to 12,000 PSI) calibrated to the exact stone diameter; breaking the loop destroys tension. | Verified master setter technical fact. |
