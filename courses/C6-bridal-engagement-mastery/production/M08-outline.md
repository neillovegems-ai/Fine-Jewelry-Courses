# Module 8 Production Outline: Sizing, Timelines, Custom and Resizing Realities

## 1. Executive Summary & Terminal Outcome
- **Terminal Outcome:** The fine jewelry bridal specialist executes infallible finger sizing assessments, manages bespoke CAD/CAM manufacturing workflows, and structures realistic production timelines with mandatory safety buffers, preventing ruined proposals and catastrophic bench resizing errors.
- **Key Operational Competencies:**
  - Measuring finger size taking into account knuckle anatomy (prominent arthritic/athletic knuckles vs conical tapering fingers).
  - Accounting for thermal expansion, pregnancy, seasonal changes, and band width differentials (wide shanks fit tighter than thin shanks).
  - Deploying mechanical sizing solutions: Speed bumps, sizing beads, butterfly springs, and horseshoe inserts.
  - Managing the custom CAD/CAM timeline: Concept sketch, 3D CAD rendering, wax/resin model try-on, lost-wax casting, stone setting, and final polish.
  - Setting firm proposal deadline buffers and educating clients on the non-resizeability of full diamond eternity bands.

## 2. Core Narrative Architecture
1. **The High Stakes of the Ring Fit:** Why an ill-fitting ring on the proposal night creates immediate distress; the difference between a ring that fits the finger base vs one that clears the knuckle.
2. **Finger Anatomy & Sizing Diagnostics:**
   - Wide vs narrow sizers (a 6mm band requires 0.5 size larger than a 1.5mm solitaire).
   - The "Prominent Knuckle Dilemma": When the knuckle is two sizes larger than the finger base; spinning rings.
   - Sizing beads, speed bumps, and custom mechanical sizers.
3. **The Custom Production Lifecycle (CAD to Collection):**
   - Step-by-step milestones: CAD design (Week 1), Wax/Resin preview (Week 2), Metal casting & cleaning (Week 3), Stone setting & rhodium (Week 4), Quality control & insurance appraisal (Week 5).
   - The "Proposal Buffer Rule": Never schedule completion on the day of the flight or proposal dinner.
4. **Resizing Realities & Technical Limitations:**
   - What can be resized easily: Plain metal solitaires (+/- 2 sizes).
   - What requires intense bench skill: Pavé shanks (risk of popping accent stones).
   - What CANNOT be resized: Full eternity bands, tension settings, alternative metals (tungsten, titanium, cobalt).
5. **Managing the Secret Sizing Mission:**
   - How to determine finger size when the proposal is a complete surprise: Borrowing an existing ring (measuring inner diameter), the sister/best friend proxy, the "Size 6.5 Safe Starting Point."
