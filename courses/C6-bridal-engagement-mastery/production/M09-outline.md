# Module 9 Production Outline: Post-Purchase: Proposal Follow-Up and Referral Asks

## 1. Executive Summary & Terminal Outcome
- **Terminal Outcome:** The fine jewelry bridal specialist executes a disciplined, discreet post-purchase relationship protocol, eliminating buyer remorse, safeguarding proposal confidentiality, providing comprehensive insurance handoffs, and converting ecstatic newly engaged couples into organic Google reviews and high-conversion peer referrals.
- **Key Operational Competencies:**
  - Managing the "Cold Feet" psychological window between ring pickup and the proposal date.
  - Executing the discreet multi-touch Proposal Countdown sequence across encrypted/preferred channels.
  - Conducting the professional appraisal and specialty jewelry insurance onboarding (e.g., Jewelers Mutual / BriteCo / homeowner floater comparison).
  - Orchestrating the "Post-Proposal Champagne Fitting" that brings the couple back into the salon within 14 days of engagement.
  - Building an active reciprocal referral flywheel with luxury wedding planners, photographers, and venues.

## 2. Core Narrative Architecture
1. **The Dangerous Post-Pickup Silence:** Why 80% of jewelry sales associates drop contact the moment payment clears, creating anxiety, second-guessing, and buyer isolation.
2. **The Proposal Countdown Protocol:**
   - Touchpoint 1 (Day +2 after pickup): Ring security and vault storage confirmation.
   - Touchpoint 2 (T-minus 7 days before proposal): Logistics, ring box concealment tips, nerves check.
   - Touchpoint 3 (The Morning Of): A private, supportive one-sentence text: *"Cheering for you today!"*
   - Touchpoint 4 (Day +1 post-proposal): The ecstatic celebration and invitation.
3. **The Insurance & Valuation Handoff:**
   - Protecting the client from immediate catastrophic loss: Why 60% of lost engagement rings disappear within the first 90 days.
   - Plain-language explanation of Retail Replacement Value vs Fair Market Value vs Cash Purchase Price.
4. **The Post-Proposal In-Salon Celebration:**
   - Champagne toast, complimentary ultrasonic clean, finger fit verification under room temperature, and official registration in the salon bridal register.
5. **The Referral & Review Engine:**
   - When and how to ask for a 5-star Google review without feeling transactional.
   - Generating user-generated content (UGC) for Instagram/TikTok with proper privacy consent.
   - Building a reciprocal wedding vendor network.
