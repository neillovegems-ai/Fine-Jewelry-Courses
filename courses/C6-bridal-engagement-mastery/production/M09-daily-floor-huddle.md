# Daily Floor Huddle Card: Module 9 (Post-Purchase: Proposal Follow-Up and Referral Asks)

### 3-Minute Floor Briefing
- **The 48-Hour Message:** Never let a bridal client walk out without sending a private confirmation text two days later: *"The diamond is magnificent, it's resting safely in your hands, and we are cheering for you!"*
- **Push Insurance Immediately:** Tell the buyer that over half of lost rings disappear in the first 90 days. Hand them the appraisal and Jewelers Mutual pamphlet before they leave.
- **Guard Proposal Confidentiality:** Mark CRM profiles "SECRET PROPOSAL" so automated receipts or emails don't spoil the surprise.
- **Host the Post-Proposal Champagne Fitting:** Bring the couple back after the engagement for champagne, ultrasonic ring cleaning, and size checks.
- **Ask for Reviews in Person:** When they are smiling with champagne in hand, ask them to leave a 5-star Google review. Then show the matching wedding band!
