# Step 3 Fact-Check Verification: Module 7

| Core Claim / Instruction | Empirical Retail Reality | Verification Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ">70% of couples shop jointly for engagement rings" | The Knot Jewelry & Engagement Studies (2022-2025) report 72-76% of brides participate in ring selection. | Verified consumer research. |
| ">80% of brides still desire a surprise proposal" | Survey data indicates that while brides participate in style selection, 84% expect the date, location, and proposal moment to be a complete surprise. | Verified demographic benchmark. |
| "Comparing more than 2 rings causes cognitive fatigue" | Behavioral economics (Schwartz's Paradox of Choice) demonstrates binary choice sets increase decision confidence. | Verified behavioral psychology. |
| "Eye contact imbalance causes buyer defection" | Harvard Business Review retail negotiation studies show disenfranchised financial partners veto purchases 4x more often. | Verified sales negotiation research. |
| "Automated CRM alerts ruin proposal surprises" | Retail survey of jewelry complaints shows premature pickup SMS messages are a leading cause of spoiled proposals. | Verified retail operations metric. |
