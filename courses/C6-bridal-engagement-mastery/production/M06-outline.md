# Module 6 Production Outline: Band Attachment and the Upgrade Path

## 1. Executive Summary & Terminal Outcome
- **Terminal Outcome:** The fine jewelry bridal specialist seamlessly integrates wedding band selection into the initial engagement ring consultation and plants the architectural seeds for long-term anniversary upgrades, elevating store attach rates from industry-average 20-25% to elite 60-70% levels.
- **Key Operational Competencies:**
  - Diagnosing engagement ring setting architecture for flush fit, under-gallery clearance, and mechanical compatibility.
  - Introducing the wedding band early during the emotional discovery phase without appearing pushy or increasing price friction.
  - Designing custom curved, contoured, and chevron bands when engagement ring heads prevent a straight band from sitting flush.
  - Articulating the store's lifetime diamond trade-in policy and anniversary milestone pathways (e.g., three-stone upgrades, eternity stackers).
  - Executing multi-ring stack consultations that anticipate future milestones (first anniversary, push gifts, vow renewals).

## 2. Core Narrative Architecture
1. **The Cost of the Forgotten Band:** Why waiting until two months before the wedding to sell the band results in lost sales, hurried compromises, and aesthetic mismatch.
2. **Architectural Ring Mechanics: Flush vs Gap:**
   - Understanding head elevation: High-set peg heads (flush capable) vs low-set cathedral or basket heads (creating a gap).
   - Explaining the "Aesthetic Gap" vs "Mechanically Damaging Friction" (rubbing diamonds against prongs).
3. **The Early Band Presentation Protocol:**
   - The exact moment to slip a band next to the engagement ring during the initial trial.
   - Scripting: *"Notice how this band locks underneath the basket. When you design the engagement ring, it’s critical to see how it lives on the finger for the rest of your life."*
4. **The Modern Stacking Phenomenon:**
   - Moving beyond the simple single band; introducing mixed metals, texture play, and the "Rule of Three" (Engagement Ring + Wedding Band + Milestone Stacker).
5. **The Lifetime Upgrade Path:**
   - Establishing diamond trade-in equity as a core luxury asset.
   - Documenting upgrade milestones in the CRM: The 5-year and 10-year diamond reset.
