# Daily Floor Huddle Card: Module 4: Natural vs. Lab-Grown

### 3-Minute Morning Briefing for the Floor Team

#### 1. Core Principle of the Day: The Values-Based Script
- Never tell a client *"Lab-grown isn't real"* (It IS real diamond carbon!).
- Never say *"Why waste money on natural?"* (Natural holds intrinsic heirloom equity!).
- **ALWAYS SAY:** *"Both are 100% genuine diamonds with identical sparkle. One is an ancient geological treasure with lasting value, and the other is a modern technological marvel that maximizes your budget. Which story resonates most with you?"*

#### 2. The Golden Rule of Resale Transparency
- Always be honest about trade-in value!
- Tell lab-grown buyers: *"Buy this for the joy of wearing it every day, not as an investment asset, because factory production makes replacement costs lower over time."*

#### 3. Quick Role-Play Practice Question
- *Customer:* "Are lab-grown diamonds fake?"
- *Associate Response:* "Not at all! A lab-grown diamond is 100% pure crystallized carbon, just like a natural diamond. It has the exact same hardness, fire, and brilliance. The only difference is where it was made: one formed deep in the earth billions of years ago, and the other was grown in a high-tech laboratory. Let's place both on the tray side-by-side so you can see them together!"
