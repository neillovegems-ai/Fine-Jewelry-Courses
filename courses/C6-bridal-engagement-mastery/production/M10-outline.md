# Module 10 Production Outline: Bridal Metrics: Tracking Your Own Conversion

## 1. Executive Summary & Terminal Outcome
- **Terminal Outcome:** The fine jewelry bridal specialist operates as an autonomous, data-driven luxury professional, tracking and optimizing individual conversion metrics, pipeline velocity, band attach percentages, and margin contributions to consistently perform in the top 1% of the industry.
- **Key Operational Competencies:**
  - Tracking the Five Core Bridal Performance Indicators: Appointment-to-Close Rate, Wedding Band Attach %, Average Engagement Ticket (AET), Pipeline Velocity, and Referral Multiplier.
  - Diagnosing pipeline leakage points (e.g., losing clients between discovery and second appointment vs losing clients at price presentation).
  - Executing the 48-Hour and 14-Day Stalled Lead Re-Engagement Protocols.
  - Balancing natural diamond gross margin dollars against lab-grown unit volume to maximize personal compensation and store profitability.
  - Conducting structured daily morning and weekly pipeline audits in the CRM.

## 2. Core Narrative Architecture
1. **The Professional Shift: Artist to Business Enterprise:** Moving away from passive hope and superstitious selling into rigorous personal analytics.
2. **The Five Essential Bridal KPIs:**
   - KPI 1: Consultation Close Rate (Target: 60%+).
   - KPI 2: Wedding Band Attach Rate (Target: 55-65%).
   - KPI 3: Average Engagement Ticket (AET).
   - KPI 4: Pipeline Cycle Time / Velocity (Target: < 21 days from discovery to deposit).
   - KPI 5: Client Advocacy Yield (Reviews & Referrals per Proposal).
3. **Pipeline Diagnostics & Funnel Leakage:**
   - Where bridal sales die: The "Think About It" trap, the "Unsent Quote" failure, and the "Ghosted Follow-Up."
   - The Stalled Lead Rescue Playbook.
4. **The Margin Strategy: Natural vs Lab-Grown Profit Dynamics:**
   - Understanding why high margin percentages on low-ticket lab diamonds can destroy total gross margin dollars; balancing product mix for sustainable career growth.
5. **The Daily 15-Minute Floor Audit:**
   - Pre-shift pipeline review, milestone tracking, and task sequencing.
