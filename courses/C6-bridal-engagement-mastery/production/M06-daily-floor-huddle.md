# Daily Floor Huddle Card: Module 6 (Band Attachment and the Upgrade Path)

### 3-Minute Floor Briefing
- **The Early Band Rule:** When showing engagement rings, always pull the matching wedding band onto the tray during the first 10 minutes. Say: *"An engagement ring is only worn alone for a few months; it lives with the wedding band for the rest of your life."*
- **Check Head Clearance:** Know your inventory. If a mounting has an elevated peg head, show a straight eternity band. If it has a low-set basket, show a contoured or curved band right away to prevent future disappointment.
- **Warn Against Diamond-on-Prong Friction:** Educate clients that mismatched online bands grind against prongs and cause lost diamonds. Casting a matching suite prevents abrasive wear.
- **Plant the Trade-In Seed at Pickup:** When handing over the completed engagement ring, explain the 100% lifetime diamond upgrade policy and celebrate their future 5-year and 10-year milestones.
- **Record the Band SKU:** Never let a bridal client leave without the exact wedding band style number and finger size recorded in their CRM profile.
