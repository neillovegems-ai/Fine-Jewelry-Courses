# Module 3 Production Outline: Shape, Setting, Metal: Guiding Without Overwhelming

## 1. Executive Summary & Terminal Outcome
- **Terminal Outcome:** The fine jewelry bridal specialist guides clients through the complex triad of diamond shape, setting architecture, and precious metal alloy using a disciplined three-tier decision funnel, eliminating choice paralysis, diagnosing optical nuances (face-up spread, L:W ratios, bow-tie effects), and aligning structural setting engineering with the wearer's daily lifestyle.
- **Key Operational Competencies:**
  - Enforcing the three-step decision funnel: Center Stone Shape &rarr; Setting Architecture &rarr; Metal Alloy.
  - Comparing fancy shapes against round brilliants based on length-to-width ratios, apparent face-up surface area ($mm^2$), and light performance.
  - Evaluating structural setting engineering: Peg Head vs Integrated Cathedral vs Bezel vs Low-Profile Floating Basket.
  - Explaining precious metal metallurgy: Platinum 950 displacement physics vs 14K/18K gold Vickers hardness and skin-tone harmony.
  - Preventing choice overload by restricting comparative viewing to a maximum of three stones or mountings at any single moment.

## 2. Core Narrative Architecture
1. **The Curse of Choice Overload:** Why showing ten trays of rings creates decision paralysis; Barry Schwartz's "Paradox of Choice" on the bridal sales floor.
2. **The 3-Step Sequential Decision Funnel:**
   - Step 1: Lock in Center Stone Shape (Determines 70% of the visual personality).
   - Step 2: Select the Setting Architecture (Defines profile height, structural support, and diamond accents).
   - Step 3: Choose the Precious Metal Alloy (Platinum vs 14K/18K Yellow, Rose, or White Gold).
3. **Diamond Shapes: Optical Geometry & Face-Up Area:**
   - Round Brilliant: GIA triple excellent benchmark, 57 facets, maximum scintillation.
   - The Elongated Suite (Oval, Emerald, Radiant, Pear, Marquise): The finger-lengthening phenomenon and why a 2.0 ct oval appears ~15% larger than a 2.0 ct round.
   - Navigating the Bow-Tie Effect: Light leakage vs dynamic facet contrast in modified brilliant cuts.
4. **Setting Architectures: Bench Engineering Meets Daily Wear:**
   - Peg Head: Fragile shear point; prone to snagging on sweaters or bending.
   - Cathedral Shank: Sloping metal arches that distribute impact energy directly into the shank.
   - Modern Bezel: Ultra-secure metal rim; ideal for healthcare workers, athletes, and active lifestyles.
   - Hidden Halo & Gallery Details: Intimate secret accents.
5. **Metallurgical Fluency: Platinum vs Gold:**
   - Platinum: High density ($21.45 g/cm^3$), natural hypoallergenic purity, displacement patina rather than metal loss.
   - Gold: 14K (harder, more scratch-resistant) vs 18K (richer saturated color, European luxury prestige).
   - Matching metal tones to skin undertones.
