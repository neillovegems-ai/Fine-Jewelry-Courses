# Morning Huddle Card: Module 2 — Discovery That Reveals the Real Purchase

## 10-Minute Team Meeting Focus

### The Core Principle
Never ask *"What's your budget?"* or *"How much do you want to spend?"* It forces clients to pick a defensive low number and turns your consultation into a price haggle. Instead, find the **Real Purchase**—the milestone, person, and sentiment behind the visit.

### 3 Questions to Ask Today
1. *"What is the wonderful celebration or milestone bringing you in today?"*
2. *"When she gets dressed up for an evening out, does she gravitate toward warm yellow gold, clean white metals, or vintage romantic designs?"*
3. *(If they're unsure)*: *"Pull out your phone and show me two or three photos of her dressed up—let's see what she loves naturally."*

### The Floor Drill: The Two-Tray Test
When calibrating price, pull two contrasting pieces on a velvet pad (e.g. $2,800 vs. $5,400):
> *"To give us a baseline of scale and presence, I brought out two options. When you see them side by side, does one feel closer to the presence you had in mind?"*
Watch their eyes and hands. They will tell you their comfort zone without a single awkward question.

### Metric Impact
Lifts **Average Ticket Value (ATV)** by 18–25% and virtually eliminates returns caused by guessing the wrong aesthetic.
