# Morning Huddle Card: Module 5: Defending Price & Gross Margins

## 10-Minute Team Meeting Focus

### The Core Principle
Stop giving away our profit! A 10% discount doesn't hurt a little, it wipes out over 50% of our store's net profit. When a customer says *"Can you do better on price?"*, do NOT cave. Execute **AARE**.

### The 4-Step AARE Formula
1. **Acknowledge:** *"I completely respect that, it is a substantial, thoughtful investment."*
2. **Anchor:** Re-state the rarity, the cut quality, and our lifetime warranty.
3. **Reframe:** Do the Cost-Per-Wear math ($12,000 over 30 years is $1.09 a day, less than a cup of coffee!).
4. **Explore:** Offer 12-month interest-free financing or a complementary appraisal instead of dropping dollars.

### The Floor Drill: The Haggler Script
When someone says: *"What's your best cash price?"*
Answer with calm pride:
> *"We price every piece fairly and transparently from day one so every client receives the absolute best value without having to play games. The price is twelve-five. Shall we size it for her?"*

### Metric Impact
Protects **45–52% Gross Margins** and eliminates the race-to-the-bottom discount habit.
