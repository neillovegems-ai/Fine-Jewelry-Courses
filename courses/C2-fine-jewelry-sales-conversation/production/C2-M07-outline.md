# Module 7 Outline: Online Comparison and the Showrooming Client

## Core Metadata
- **Course:** C2 The Fine Jewelry Sales Conversation
- **Track:** Foundations (P1, P2, P3)
- **Module:** 7 of 10
- **Anchor Sources:** GIA Cut Research (Reinitz/Moses), INSTORE Magazine Showrooming Studies, Jewelers of America Bench Standards, AGS Light Performance.

## Learning Objectives
1. Eliminate panic and defensiveness when clients reference online competitor pricing.
2. Articulate the difference between two-dimensional grading reports and three-dimensional optical life.
3. Identify the "B-Grade" optical defects common on discount e-commerce sites (milky clouds, undertones).
4. Master the Four Pillars of the Physical Salon Advantage (Optics, Ergonomics, Provenance, Bench Covenant).
5. Execute the Side-by-Side Optical Challenge to convert hesitant shoppers.
