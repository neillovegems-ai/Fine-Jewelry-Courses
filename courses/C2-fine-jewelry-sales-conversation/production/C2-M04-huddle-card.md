# Morning Huddle Card: Module 4: Buying Signals & Silence Discipline

## 10-Minute Team Meeting Focus

### The Core Principle
Stop talking past the close! When a customer decides to buy, extra words create doubt. The moment you see a buying signal, your presentation is finished, step back, shut up, and let them buy.

### Top 3 Buying Signals to Watch Today
1. **The Pronoun Shift:** They stop saying *"that ring"* and start saying *"my ring"* or *"how will this look with my watch?"*
2. **The 5-Second Mirror Stare:** They stop looking down at their hand and gaze silently into the mirror.
3. **The Logistical Question:** *"How long does sizing take?"* or *"Can I shower with this?"*

### The Floor Drill: The 5-Second Price Rule
When you quote a price today:
1. Say the number clearly: *"That diamond is twelve thousand four hundred."*
2. **Do not say another word.** Zip your lips for 5 full seconds.
3. Keep gentle eye contact and smile. The person who speaks first determines the outcome!

### Metric Impact
Shortens transaction times by **15 minutes** and prevents the devastating lost sale caused by introducing unnecessary doubts.
