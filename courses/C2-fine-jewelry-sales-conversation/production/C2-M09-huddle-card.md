# Morning Huddle Card: Module 9: The Three Luxury Closing Modalities

## 10-Minute Team Meeting Focus

### The Core Principle
Stop waiting for customers to beg to buy! 60% of sales die because associates are afraid to ask. Asking for the sale isn't pushy, it's leadership. Help the customer cross the **Decision Threshold**.

### The 3 Ways to Close Today
1. **The Assumptive Close:** Don't ask to buy; ask to size:
   > *"Why don't we do this: let's take Sarah's exact ring size right now so our workshop can have this ready for Saturday."*
2. **The Alternative Choice:** Never ask yes/no; give two positive choices:
   > *"Would you prefer the clean platinum mounting, or does the warm yellow gold suit her better?"*
3. **The Emotional Milestone Close:** Remind them of the love/sacrifice:
   > *"She stood by you through five years of building your practice. Let's make this the anniversary she talks about for the rest of her life."*

### If They Hesitate
Smile, step back, and say:
> *"Of course! I never want you moving faster than feels 100% right. Is it the style, the diamond, or the investment level giving you pause?"*

### Metric Impact
Lifts overall closing ratio from **18% to over 38%** across qualified floor presentations.
