# Knowledge Quiz: Course C2, Module 9 (Three Ways to Ask for the Sale)

**1. According to sales research from NASP and retail audits, approximately what percentage of sales interactions end without the associate asking for the sale?**
- A) Less than 10%.
- B) Approximately 25%.
- C) Over 60%. (Correct)
- D) Exactly 100%.

**2. Why do luxury clients often experience hesitation at the "Decision Threshold"?**
- A) They are required to obtain a permit from the city.
- B) High-ticket luxury decisions trigger autonomic cognitive anxiety and fear of making an expensive mistake. (Correct)
- C) They want to see the jeweler's business license.
- D) They do not like jewelry.

**3. What is the fundamental mechanism of the Assumptive Close?**
- A) Forcing the customer to sign a contract before leaving the counter.
- B) Assuming the customer has emotionally decided to purchase, and transitioning smoothly into the next operational logistics step (sizing, packaging, delivery). (Correct)
- C) Assuming the customer has no money.
- D) Threatening to sell the diamond to someone else.

**4. How does the Alternative Choice Close eliminate the customer's decision paralysis?**

- B) It requires the customer to choose between two different jewelry stores.
- C) It offers a 50% discount on the second item.
- D) It asks the customer to flip a coin.

**5. What are the three steps of the Emotional Milestone Close?**
- A) Discount, Financing, and Cash.
- B) Recall the Story, Anchor the Meaning, and Issue the Invitation to Celebrate. (Correct)
- C) Carat, Color, and Clarity.
- D) Greeting, Discovery, and Follow-Up.

**6. Which of the following is an example of an Assumptive Close?**
- A) "Do you want to buy this ring today?"
- B) "Let's take your exact ring measurement right now so our master workshop can have this ready for your weekend proposal." (Correct)
- C) "If you don't buy this today, the price goes up tomorrow."
- D) "Can I have your credit card?"

**7. Why does the Emotional Milestone Close effectively overcome price resistance?**

- C) Because it promises guaranteed investment returns.

**8. If an associate attempts an Assumptive Close and the client says "Wait, I'm not ready to size it yet," what should the associate do?**

- B) Smile warmly, validate their hesitation immediately, and gently isolate the underlying concern using the Three-Category Choice. (Correct)
- C) Call the police.
- D) Offer a 30% discount immediately.

**9. Why do luxury consumers want sales associates to lead them during the closing phase?**
- A) They are too tired to speak.
- B) They seek confidence, validation, and professional guidance to overcome the natural fear of high-ticket financial commitment. (Correct)
- C) Store policies require customers to obey associates.
- D) They want someone to carry their shopping bags.

**10. What is an ideal Alternative Choice Close for an anxious gift buyer who is worried his partner will discover the ring at home?**
- A) "Would you prefer to hide it in your car, or in your garage?"
- B) "Would you like to take the ring home with you today, or keep it securely locked in our vault until the morning of your anniversary?" (Correct)
