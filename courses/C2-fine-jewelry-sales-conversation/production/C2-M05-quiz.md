**5. Which of the following represents the "Anchor" step in the AARE framework?**

**7. Why is offering preferred client financing (e.g. 12 months 0% APR) an effective response to price resistance?**

- B) It charges the customer an exorbitant interest penalty.

**8. When an aggressive haggler asks "What's your best cash price?", what is the recommended master response?**

**9. Why is offering a complementary merchandise attachment (e.g. travel case, appraisal, pearl studs) preferable to giving a cash discount?**

**10. What does the "E" stand for in the AARE objection framework?**
