# Knowledge Quiz: Course C2, Module 2 (Discovery Questions)

**1. Why is asking "What's your budget?" in the early minutes of a sales conversation considered a major floor mistake?**
- A) It is illegal under FTC retail disclosure regulations.
- B) It prompts clients to state an artificially low defensive number and turns consultation into price negotiation. (Correct)
- C) It encourages the customer to request store financing immediately.
- D) It violates GIA diamond appraisal standards.

**2. What is meant by "The Real Purchase" in luxury fine jewelry sales?**
- A) The wholesale cost of goods minus trade discounts.
- B) The physical weight of the gold and the carat weight of the center stone.
- C) The underlying emotional milestone, sentiment, or life chapter being honored by the piece. (Correct)
- D) The signed credit application approved at the register.

**3. What is the first sequence of the Three-Sequence Discovery Funnel?**
- A) Financial Qualification and Credit Check.
- B) The Occasion and Emotional Driver. (Correct)
- C) GIA Diamond Certificate Comparison.
- D) Metal Alloy Durability Testing.

**4. When a gift buyer says "I have no idea what style she likes," what is the most effective immediate floor move?**
- A) Show the most expensive item in the vault to establish prestige.
- B) Ask him to bring his wife into the store next week instead.
- C) Invite him to show you two or three smartphone photos of her dressed up for social occasions. (Correct)
- D) Hand him a gemological textbook and explain jewelry history.

**5. How does the Comparative Two-Tray Presentation discover a client's price parameters?**
- A) It requires the client to sign a financial disclosure form before trying on rings.
- B) It displays two distinct investment tiers side-by-side, allowing physical reactions and visual preferences to reveal their comfort zone. (Correct)
- C) It compares a mined diamond directly against a cubic zirconia simulant.
- D) It tests whether the customer has cash or a credit card.

**6. Which question is the best replacement for the stale retail phrase "What style does she like?"**
- A) "Do you want prong set or channel set?"
- B) "If you had to describe her signature style in three words, would she be classic, modern, or vintage romantic?" (Correct)
- C) "Is she picky about jewelry?"
- D) "Does she know you are shopping today?"

**7. Why is exploring the recipient's daily profession and lifestyle essential during discovery?**
- A) To determine the client's annual household income.
- B) To ensure the structural ergonomics and durability of the setting match her daily activities (e.g. healthcare, athletics, desk work). (Correct)
- C) To calculate state sales tax accurately.
- D) To verify employment for an in-store credit line.

**8. If a client shopping for an engagement ring appears visibly anxious, how should the associate de-escalate their fear?**
- A) Tell them that diamond prices increase at the end of the month.
- B) Step away and let them wander the showroom alone for twenty minutes.
- C) Normalize the overwhelm, explain house exchange and sizing policies, and reassure them that you will help them look like a hero. (Correct)
- D) Urge them to buy a pre-set stock ring immediately to save time.

**9. When a couple enters with conflicting aesthetic tastes (e.g. one wants minimalist, the other wants ornate), the associate should:**
- A) Agree with whoever holds the credit card.
- B) Tell the couple to go home and decide before returning.
- C) Validate both aesthetic viewpoints and propose design solutions that harmonize both elements (e.g. clean solitaire lines with a hidden pavé collar). (Correct)
- D) Suggest they buy two separate rings.

**10. What is the recommended reaction when a client walks in and immediately asks "How much per carat is this diamond?"**
- A) Refuse to answer until they provide their name and phone number.
- B) Transparently quote the price immediately, then pivot to what creates the stone's optical beauty and discovery context. (Correct)
- C) Tell them price doesn't matter in fine jewelry.
- D) Call the store manager over to handle the confrontation.
