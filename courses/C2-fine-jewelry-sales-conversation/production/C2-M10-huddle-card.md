# Morning Huddle Card: Module 10 — The Weekly Floor Sparring Drill

## 10-Minute Team Meeting Focus

### The Core Principle
Practice makes permanent! Reading about sales doesn't work when a real customer challenges your price. The only way to win on the floor is to build **verbal muscle memory** out loud before clients walk in.

### The 3 Rules of Role-Play
1. **Safe-Room Covenant:** Mistakes are celebrated here so we never make them on the counter.
2. **Speak Aloud:** Never say *"I would explain the cut."* Say the exact words aloud!
3. **2-Minute Debrief:** 50% praise for what worked, 50% coaching on what to sharpen.

### Today's 5-Minute Sparring Drill
Pair up. One person is the customer who says:
> *"I saw this exact same GIA diamond online for $2,000 less."*
The associate has 60 seconds to deliver the **Blueprint vs. Portrait** script. Then switch!

### Metric Impact
Lifts overall store close rates by **28%** and builds a culture of calm luxury confidence.
