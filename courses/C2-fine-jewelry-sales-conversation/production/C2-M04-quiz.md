# Knowledge Quiz: Course C2, Module 4 (Buying Signals & Silence)

**1. What is the sales floor mistake known as "Talking Past the Close"?**
- A) Speaking too loudly in a quiet luxury showroom.
- B) Continuing to deliver sales presentations and technical details after the client has already mentally decided to purchase, inadvertently manufacturing doubts. (Correct)
- C) Quoting a price without adding local sales tax.
- D) Failing to offer a beverage to an accompanying guest.

**2. What is the "Possessive Pronoun Shift"?**
- A) A grammar mistake made by inexperienced retail associates.
- B) The linguistic transition where a client shifts from objective words ("that ring") to possessive words ("my band", "our anniversary"), signaling psychological ownership. (Correct)
- C) Using third-person formal pronouns when addressing high-net-worth clients.
- D) Translating European jewelry brochures into English.

**3. When a client slips a ring on and gazes silently into the counter mirror for more than three seconds, the associate should:**
- A) Immediately list the diamond's table and depth percentages.
- B) Remain completely silent and allow the client to experience the emotional reverie of their reflection. (Correct)
- C) Ask if they want to see a different cut.
- D) Take the ring back and return it to the showcase.

**4. What is the recommended associate behavior immediately after quoting a high-ticket price?**
- A) Immediately apologize for the price and offer a 15% discount.
- B) Suggest the customer apply for store financing before they react.
- C) State the price clearly with calm eye contact and maintain silence until the client speaks. (Correct)
- D) Quickly explain wholesale diamond price indexes.

**5. What is the primary purpose of the "Two-Foot Step-Back Protocol"?**
- A) To check the store security monitors for suspicious activity.
- B) To physically relieve pressure on the client and communicate that they are in full control of their decision. (Correct)
- C) To allow another sales associate to take over the transaction.
- D) To comply with fire safety clearance codes behind the showcase.

**6. Which of the following is an example of a verbal logistical buying signal?**
- A) "Where is the nearest restroom?"
- B) "If we decided on this today, how long would custom sizing take?" (Correct)
- C) "Who is the owner of this jewelry store?"
- D) "Why are diamonds so expensive?"

**7. Why does nervous retail sales staff frequently suffer from horror vacui (fear of silence)?**
- A) They are required by management to speak at least 200 words per minute.
- B) They mistakenly equate conversational silence with failure or awkwardness, rather than recognizing it as necessary cognitive decision-making space. (Correct)
- C) They want to impress the store security guard.
- D) They have memorized too many product codes.

**8. If a client says "This is way over my budget," but continues stroking the ring and looking into the mirror with relaxed shoulders, what is happening?**
- A) The client is planning to shoplift the ring.
- B) Their logical brain is expressing budget caution, but their subconscious body language indicates powerful emotional attachment and desire. (Correct)
- C) The ring is physically stuck on their finger.
- D) They want the associate to show them silver jewelry instead.

**9. How should an associate test the purchase intent of an overly enthusiastic browser who has tried on numerous items?**
- A) Ask for their driver's license and credit card immediately.
- B) Ask a gentle reality-testing question: "If we find the piece you love today, are you hoping to take it home this afternoon, or are we curating your official holiday wishlist?" (Correct)
- C) Tell them they are only allowed to try on three pieces per visit.
- D) Ignore them and assist another customer.

**10. When an accompanying partner looks at the client and says "Honey, it looks unbelievable on you," what should the associate do?**
- A) Bring out three more rings to show other options.
- B) Agree warmly and immediately transition toward closing documentation and sizing. (Correct)
- C) Explain how platinum compares to white gold.
- D) Step away to take a phone call.
