# Morning Huddle Card: Module 6 — Winning the "I'll Think About It" Client

## 10-Minute Team Meeting Focus

### The Core Principle
Never let an undecided customer walk out with just a business card! 92% never come back. When they say *"I need to think about it"*, validate them, isolate the real issue, and text them a **Digital Spec Sheet**.

### The 3-Category Isolation Question
When they hesitate, say with a warm smile:
> *"Of course, take all the time you need! In my experience, when someone loves a piece but wants to think, it's usually: 1) the style, 2) the diamond, or 3) the investment level. Which one is lingering in your mind?"*

### The Floor Drill: The Digital Spec Sheet
Never ask *"Can I have your number?"* Say:
> *"While you were looking, I took two high-res photos and a photo of the GIA cert. What's your cell number? I'll text you the digital portfolio right now so you have it in your phone."*

### Metric Impact
Lifts the "Be-Back" conversion rate from **8% to over 34%** and builds an active CRM pipeline.
