# Module 5 Outline: Price Objections Without Discounting

## Core Metadata
- **Course:** C2 The Fine Jewelry Sales Conversation
- **Track:** Foundations (P1, P2, P3)
- **Module:** 5 of 10
- **Anchor Sources:** INSTORE Magazine Profit Benchmarks, Shane Decker sales archives, Terry Sisco Exsellerate, Harvard Program on Negotiation.

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand the devastating net-profit impact of top-line retail discounting.
2. Master the four-step AARE Objection Recovery Framework (Acknowledge, Anchor, Reframe, Explore).
3. Execute the Cost-Per-Wear formula to reframe lump-sum sticker shock.
4. Utilize margin-preserving alternatives (financing, spec shifts, value-add attachments).
5. Defend transparent pricing against aggressive hagglers with calm luxury stature.
