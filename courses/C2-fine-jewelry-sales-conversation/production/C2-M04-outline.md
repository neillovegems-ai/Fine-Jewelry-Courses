# Module 4 Outline: Buying Signals and When to Stop Talking

## Core Metadata
- **Course:** C2 The Fine Jewelry Sales Conversation
- **Track:** Foundations (P1, P2, P3)
- **Module:** 4 of 10
- **Anchor Sources:** Shane Decker sales seminars, INSTORE Magazine, Allan Pease body language research, Harvard Program on Negotiation.

## Learning Objectives
1. Eliminate the destructive habit of "talking past the close."
2. Identify the 18 diagnostic verbal, non-verbal, and linguistic buying signals.
3. Master the Possessive Pronoun Shift to detect psychological ownership.
4. Execute the Sacred Silence Discipline and the Two-Foot Step-Back Protocol.
5. Transition smoothly from buying signals to closing logistics without pressure.

## Module Structure
1. Self-Critique Rubric (Step 2 SOP)

   - Category A: Physical & Non-Verbal (Mirror gaze, ambient light, tactile stroking)
   - Category B: Verbal & Logistical (Sizing timelines, care questions, packaging)
   - Category C: Linguistic (The Possessive Pronoun Shift)
5. The Sacred Silence Discipline (The 5-Second Pause & Price Drop Rule)
6. The Physical Retreat: The 2-Foot Step-Back Protocol
7. Graceful Transition Bridges (Worked Dialogues)
8. Summary Matrix: The Buying Signal Playbook
9. Floor Edge Cases (The Flirtatious Browser, Poker Face, Contradictory Signals)

11. 10-Question Self-Check & Trade References
