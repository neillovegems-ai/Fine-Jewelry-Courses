# Module 2 Outline: Discovery: The Questions That Reveal the Real Purchase

## Core Metadata
- **Course:** C2 The Fine Jewelry Sales Conversation
- **Track:** Foundations (P1, P2, P3)
- **Module:** 2 of 10
- **Anchor Sources:** Terry Sisco Exsellerate, INSTORE Magazine, Shane Decker archives, NRF Luxury Benchmarks.

## Learning Objectives
1. Eliminate the question "What's your budget?" from client interactions.
2. Uncover the emotional motivator ("The Real Purchase") behind surface requests.
3. Master the 3-Sequence Discovery Funnel (Occasion, Lifestyle, Investment Framing).
4. Utilize comparative presentation trays to determine financial comfort boundaries.
5. De-escalate anxious and secretive luxury buyers.
