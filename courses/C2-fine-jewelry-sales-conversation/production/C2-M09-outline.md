# Module 9 Outline: Three Ways to Ask for the Sale

## Core Metadata
- **Course:** C2 The Fine Jewelry Sales Conversation
- **Track:** Foundations (P1, P2, P3)
- **Module:** 9 of 10
- **Anchor Sources:** NASP Closing Benchmarks, Harvard Program on Negotiation, INSTORE Magazine, Shane Decker archives.

## Learning Objectives
1. Eliminate closing paralysis and the fear of rejection.
2. Master the Assumptive Close (operational next steps).
3. Execute the Alternative Choice Close (eliminating binary dilemma).
4. Deliver the Emotional Milestone Close (anchoring on the real purchase).
5. De-escalate with total grace when a client hesitates.
