# C1 Diamond & Gemstone Fluency for the Sales Floor

**Track:** Foundations
**Personas:** P1, P2, P3
**10 articles, 8-10 learner hours (original estimate; expanded content may extend actual study time)**
**Research anchor:** Reference §4 table 1 (R1), §2 Tier 1

## Terminal outcomes

The learner can:
- Explain the 4Cs in plain language and connect each to visible differences in the case
- Read a GIA or AGS report line-by-line with a client
- Disclose treatments, lab-grown origin and simulants
- Correctly identify the ten most-sold colored stones and their care requirements
- Explain precious-metal marks, purity and alloys
- Answer the fifteen most common product questions without a manager

**Assessment:** 40-question self-test plus a manager-observed "explain this stone" demonstration.
**KPI link:** close rate on diamond inquiries, protection-plan attach.
