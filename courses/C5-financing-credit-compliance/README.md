# C5 Financing, Credit & Compliance at the Counter

**Track:** Core Foundations &bull; Financial & Regulatory Compliance Track (Track 3)  
**Personas:** P1 (New Associate), P4 (Bridal Specialist), P7 (Store Leader / Boutique Director)  
**6 modules, 4–6 learner hours (~25,000 words total)**  
**Research anchor:** Reference R8 (Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Career-and-Skills-Reference.md Section 3.4 & Section 4 Table 8), Fine-Jewelry-Retail-Prompt-Kit.md lines 113–120, Synchrony Retail Orientation Guide (PDF), Synchrony Luxury Credit Program Architecture, CFPB Retail Installment & BNPL Interpretive Rules (May 2024), Truth in Lending Act (TILA / Regulation Z - 12 CFR Part 1026), Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA / Regulation B - 12 CFR Part 1002), Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA - 15 U.S.C. § 1681), Jewelers Vigilance Committee (JVC) Essential Guides, and FTC Guides for the Jewelry, Precious Metals, and Pewter Industries (16 CFR Part 23).

---

## Terminal Outcomes

The learner can:
- Explain the tripartite economic mechanics of private-label retail financing, revolving lines, merchant discount rates (MDR), and instant automated POS underwriting cascades.
- Present financing seamlessly during Stage 2 Discovery as an empowering budget enablement concierge tool, utilizing dual-pricing quotes and overcoming debt hesitation with dignity.
- Master the statutory distinction between true 0% APR and Deferred Interest, calculate retroactive interest penalties, expose the statement minimum payment trap, and apply the "N - 1 Rule" for guaranteed safe payoff.
- Eliminate prohibited verbal promises regarding approvals, terms, and credit scores under TILA Regulation Z and ECOA Regulation B, while executing the Dignified Adverse Action protocol on declined applications.
- Compare traditional in-house layaway bailment custody with modern Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) fintech platforms and closed-end retail installment sales contracts (RISC) under UCC Article 9.
- Apply FTC Jewelry Guides (16 CFR Part 23) and JVC compliance standards to diamond origins (natural vs lab-grown vs simulant), gemstone treatments (permanence, care, value), precious metal markings (10k gold, platinum alloys, vermeil), and eliminate deceptive counter appraisal claims.

**Assessment:** Scenario quiz on permitted statements and complete retail compliance audit.  
**KPI link:** Financing attach rate (>35%), average order value lift (+30%–50%), zero regulatory/FTC compliance breaches.  
**Production Media Manifest:** [C5-media-manifest.md](production/C5-media-manifest.md) (30 live, verified figures & regulatory frameworks).

## Production Rules & Six-Step SOP

Each module is constructed per the project's rigorous six-step standard:
1. **Outline & Pedagogical Blueprint** (`production/C5-Mxx-outline.md`)
2. **Comprehensive Long-Form Article** (`articles/Mxx-*.md`) with no-cap word depth (~3,500–5,000 words), statutory references, and verbatim dialogues.
3. **Step 2 Self-Critique & Rubric** (Scored across outcome alignment, factual discipline, floor readiness, voice, scannability, persona fit).
4. **Fact-Check Table** (`production/C5-Mxx-factcheck.md`) documenting statutory citations (TILA, ECOA, FCRA, CFPB, FTC 16 CFR Part 23).
5. **Repurposing Artifacts** (`production/C5-Mxx-huddle-card.md` and `production/C5-Mxx-quiz.md` with 10 questions).
6. **Option B Visual Enrichment** (5 figures/frameworks per module with responsive luxury card styling, published to WordPress and Tutor LMS).
