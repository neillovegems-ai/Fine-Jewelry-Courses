# Daily Floor Huddle Card: Module 3: Deferred Interest & Plain-Language Disclosures

### 3-Minute Morning Briefing for the Floor Team

#### 1. Core Principle of the Day: The N - 1 Rule
- When setting up a **12-Month Deferred Interest** plan, never divide by 12. **Divide by 11!**
- *Example:* On a $5,500 purchase, $\$5,500 / 11 = \$500/	ext{month}$.
- *Why:* The client pays off the entire balance in Month 11, giving them a 30-day buffer against bank holidays, clearing delays, or payment glitches. Zero penalty risk!

#### 2. The Golden Disclosure Rule
- Never let a customer leave thinking they have a true 0% loan.
- Always explain: *"This is deferred interest. If it's paid off within 12 months, it's completely interest-free. But if there's even a dollar left over, the bank charges back-interest from today. That’s why we set up your schedule for $500 a month to pay it off early!"*

#### 3. Quick Role-Play Practice Question
- *Customer:* "My statement says my minimum payment is only $85. Can I just pay that?"
- *Associate Response:* "I'm so glad you asked! The bank prints that $85 minimum because they are legally required to, but if you only pay $85, you will only pay off about a third of the ring by month 12, which triggers the retroactive interest charge. To keep your purchase 100% interest-free, you want to pay the $500 we wrote down on your receipt. That clears it completely before the deadline!"
