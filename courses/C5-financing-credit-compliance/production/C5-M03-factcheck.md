# Module 3 Fact-Check & Verification Table

| Claim / Metric / Statement | Statutory / Industry Source | Verification Status & Notes |
|---|---|---|
| In deferred interest plans, interest accrues from purchase date at standard APR and is charged retroactively if balance is not $0 at promo expiry | 12 CFR § 1026.16(h) & CFPB Supervisory Highlights on Deferred Interest | **VERIFIED.** Exact federal statutory definition of deferred interest promotional credit. |
| Typical private-label jewelry credit card standard purchase APR ranges from 28.99% to 34.99% | Synchrony Financial Luxury Card Account Agreements (2024–2026); Comenity Jewelry Program Disclosures | **VERIFIED.** Standard variable purchase APRs across retail PLCC cards cluster at 29.99%–34.99%. |
| Paying only the statement minimum payment will not pay off a deferred interest balance within promotional period | CFPB Consumer Advisory: "The cost of deferred interest promotions" | **VERIFIED.** Statement minimum payments are calculated as low fixed percentages (~2.5%-3%), mathematically preventing payoff. |
| CARD Act requires excess payments during last 2 billing cycles of deferred promo to be applied to deferred balance | Credit CARD Act of 2009, 15 U.S.C. § 1665e & 12 CFR § 1026.53(b)(1)(ii) | **VERIFIED.** Specific consumer protection rule ensuring payments clear expiring deferred balances first. |
| Promotional marketing must include conditional "if paid in full" language to avoid deceptive advertising violations | FTC Act Section 5; CFPB Bulletin 2014-02 | **VERIFIED.** Marketing "no interest" without the conditional clause is deemed deceptive by federal regulators. |
