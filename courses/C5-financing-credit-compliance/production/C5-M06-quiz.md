# Module 6 Assessment: FTC Guides and Product Representation for Sellers

### 10-Question Comprehensive Examination with Rationales

#### Q1: Under FTC 16 CFR § 23.25, what is the legal meaning of the word "diamond" when used alone without any qualification or adjective?
- A) Any transparent, sparkling gemstone
- B) An un-treated natural mineral formed entirely in the earth consisting essentially of pure carbon crystallized in the isometric system
- C) A synthetic cubic zirconia
- D) Any lab-created diamond produced after 2018
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Under federal statutory guidelines, the unqualified word "diamond" legally signifies a natural, earth-mined stone. Any man-made or synthetic origin must be explicitly qualified.

#### Q2: Which of the following descriptors is strictly PROHIBITED by the FTC when describing a laboratory-grown diamond to a consumer?
- A) "Laboratory-grown diamond"
- B) "Natural diamond"
- C) "Laboratory-created diamond"
- D) "[Manufacturer Name]-created diamond"
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* 16 CFR § 23.25 explicitly prohibits describing any laboratory-created stone as "natural," "real," or "genuine," as this deceptively implies an earth-mined origin.

#### Q3: Under the National Stamping Act and FTC Guides, what is the minimum karatage required for an item to be described or stamped as "gold" in the United States?
- A) 8 Karats
- B) 9 Karats
- C) 10 Karats (41.7% pure gold)
- D) 14 Karats
*Correct Answer:* C
*Rationale:* In the United States, 10K is the legal minimum threshold for gold. Articles under 10K cannot be advertised or marked as "gold."

#### Q4: To legally describe a piece of jewelry as "Vermeil" under 16 CFR § 23.4(b), what three statutory requirements must be satisfied?
- A) Solid 14K gold base, rhodium plating, 1 micron thickness
- B) Sterling silver base (92.5% silver), coated with at least 10K gold, with a minimum coating thickness of 2.5 microns (100 millionths of an inch)
- C) Copper base, 24K gold flash plating, 0.5 micron thickness
- D) Stainless steel base with gold PVD coating
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Vermeil is strictly defined by law as heavy gold plating (minimum 2.5 microns of 10k+ gold) over a genuine sterling silver base.

#### Q5: Under FTC 16 CFR § 23.22, when is a retailer legally REQUIRED to disclose a gemstone treatment to a customer?
- A) Only if the customer specifically asks about treatments
- B) If the treatment is non-permanent, requires special cleaning/care, or significantly affects the market value of the gemstone
- C) Only if the stone costs over $100,000
- D) Never; treatments are considered trade secrets
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* The FTC mandates disclosure if the treatment is impermanent, creates special care requirements, or impacts market value.

#### Q6: Why is calling a lead-glass filled composite ruby simply a "natural ruby" an illegal deceptive trade practice?
- A) Because lead-glass filled rubies are radioactive
- B) Because a composite ruby contains substantial amounts of glass filler that can be destroyed by heat or household cleaners, and untreated rubies command exponentially higher market value
- C) Because rubies can only be sold in Asia
- D) Because glass is not transparent
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Lead-glass rubies are fragile composite materials that degrade easily and have minimal market value compared to untreated natural corundum; full disclosure is mandatory.

#### Q7: An associate tells a customer: "Buy this diamond ring for $4,000 today, because our appraisal says it’s worth $8,000, so you're making a 100% profit the second you walk out." How is this statement classified under federal law?
- A) A standard, acceptable sales closing technique
- B) Deceptive pricing and consumer fraud under FTC Guides (16 CFR Part 233) and state unfair trade practices laws
- C) A legally binding contract to buy the ring back for $8,000
- D) Permitted under Truth in Lending Act
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Using inflated insurance replacement appraisals to claim instant customer equity or financial profit is deceptive and unlawful.

#### Q8: Under FTC rules, can an item containing 600 parts per thousand pure platinum be marked simply as "Platinum"?
- A) Yes, anything over 500 parts can be called platinum
- B) No; under FTC rules, platinum alloys between 500 and 850 parts per thousand must clearly disclose both metals (e.g., "600 Plat / 400 Irid" or "600 Plat / 400 Ruthenium")
- C) Only if it is sold to an appraiser
- D) Yes, but only in California
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* 16 CFR § 23.6 mandates that platinum alloys between 500 and 850 parts per thousand must state the specific proportional alloy breakdown.

#### Q9: What does "CTTW" represent on a fine jewelry sales invoice, and what is the legal requirement regarding its disclosure?
- A) Center stone true weight; applies only to solitaires
- B) Carat Total Weight; when multiple stones are present, the retailer must state total weight and adhere to FTC fraction tolerances
- C) Certified treated total weight; applies only to heated stones
- D) Color, Tone, and Tint Weight
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* CTTW stands for Carat Total Weight, representing the combined weight of all diamonds in the piece, subject to statutory fraction rounding guidelines.

#### Q10: What is the primary role of the Jewelers Vigilance Committee (JVC) in the fine jewelry industry?
- A) To sell jewelry insurance policies
- B) To act as the legal compliance watchdog, educating retailers, monitoring advertising, defending industry integrity, and assisting in the enforcement of FTC Guides and anti-money-laundering laws
- C) To manufacture lab-grown diamonds
- D) To set diamond commodity prices in Antwerp
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* The JVC is the non-profit legal guardian of the jewelry trade, providing regulatory education and promoting legal compliance across the supply chain.
