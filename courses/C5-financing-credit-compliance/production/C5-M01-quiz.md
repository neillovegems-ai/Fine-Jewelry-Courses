# Module 1 Assessment: How Retail Financing Actually Works

### 10-Question Comprehensive Examination with Rationales

#### Q1: In a private-label retail jewelry financing transaction, who assumes the financial risk if the consumer subsequently defaults on their payments?
- A) The sales associate who facilitated the sale
- B) The retail jewelry store owner
- C) The third-party issuing bank (e.g., Synchrony Bank)
- D) The credit bureau (Equifax/Experian)
*Correct Answer:* C
*Rationale:* Under standard private-label merchant agreements, the issuing bank underwrites the consumer credit risk and assumes 100% of default risk once the transaction is funded, provided the merchant followed valid identification and processing protocols.

#### Q2: What is the "Merchant Discount Rate" (MDR)?
- A) A discount offered to consumers who pay in physical cash
- B) The percentage fee deducted by the issuing bank from the gross transaction amount to subsidize promotional financing
- C) The markdown taken on aged estate inventory
- D) The interest rate charged to the consumer after a promo period ends
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* The MDR (dealer fee) is the fee the retailer pays to the lending bank to provide subsidized promotional financing (e.g., 6, 12, or 24 months 0% or deferred interest).

#### Q3: A customer wants to buy an $8,000 diamond ring. Which scenario yields the HIGHEST gross dollar margin for the jewelry store?
- A) Giving the customer an 8% cash discount ($640 discount)
- B) Selling the ring at full $8,000 retail price on a 12-month promo plan with a 5.0% MDR ($400 fee)
- C) Giving the customer a 10% courtesy discount ($800 discount)
- D) Offering a free $700 wedding band to close the cash deal
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Absorbing a 5.0% MDR on $8,000 costs the store $400, leaving $7,600 net revenue. An 8% cash discount reduces revenue to $7,360 ($240 less profit). Financing preserves pricing integrity and cash margin.

#### Q4: Why is an open-end revolving credit line (PLCC) strategically superior to a closed-end installment loan for long-term clienteling?
- A) Open-end revolving lines do not require the bank to check credit
- B) Open-end revolving lines automatically reload as balances are paid down, providing an approved credit reserve for future anniversary and holiday purchases without re-application
- C) Closed-end loans are illegal in luxury retail
- D) Revolving lines have lower interest rates for consumers who default
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* An open-end revolving credit card remains open and active. As the client pays down their balance, their available credit reloads, creating frictionless buying power for future gift-giving occasions.

#### Q5: Under federal banking privacy guidelines and the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), what information is provided to the store associate when an application is declined?
- A) The customer's exact FICO score and delinquent accounts
- B) The customer's debt-to-income ratio
- C) Only an automated notice that the bank is unable to approve the application at this time and that an Adverse Action letter will be mailed to the applicant
- D) The customer's total outstanding credit card debt across other banks
*Correct Answer:* C
*Rationale:* To protect consumer privacy, federal law prohibits lending banks from sharing credit report details or adverse action reasons with store personnel. The bank must mail or electronically deliver an Adverse Action notice directly to the consumer.

#### Q6: How quickly does an issuing bank typically settle funds into a retail jeweler's commercial bank account after an authorized transaction is finalized?
- A) 30 to 60 days
- B) Immediately at midnight of the same minute
- C) 24 to 48 business hours via ACH
- D) Only after the consumer makes their first three monthly payments
*Correct Answer:* C
*Rationale:* Private-label credit card providers settle transactions within 1 to 2 business days (24–48 hours) via direct ACH deposit, ensuring strong merchant liquidity.

#### Q7: What is the primary operational advantage of using "Text-to-Apply" or QR code mobile applications at the jewelry counter?
- A) It allows associates to bypass bank underwriting
- B) It prevents the client from reading the terms and conditions
- C) It protects consumer privacy by allowing the client to enter their Social Security Number and income directly on their personal mobile device without sharing sensitive PII with store staff
- D) It eliminates the need for the merchant to pay an MDR fee
*Correct Answer:* C
*Rationale:* Direct-to-device digital applications ensure high-security compliance and consumer comfort by keeping Personally Identifiable Information (PII) off store counters and out of sales staff hands.

#### Q8: What does a "Secondary Financing Waterfall" accomplish in a retail store?
- A) It routes declined prime applications automatically to near-prime secondary lenders, increasing total counter approval rates
- B) It reduces the retail price of the jewelry automatically by 20%
- C) It transfers inventory from store stock to consignment
- D) It splits the purchase across four different credit cards simultaneously
*Correct Answer:* A
*Rationale:* A credit waterfall routes applicants who do not qualify for Tier 1 prime credit to secondary near-prime lenders, maximizing customer approvals without forcing the customer to re-enter their information.

#### Q9: Which of the following customer demographics benefits most from private-label jewelry financing?
- A) Only customers who have zero savings and bad credit
- B) High-credit-quality customers who want to preserve liquid investment cash or emergency savings while making planned monthly payments
- C) Customers who plan to declare bankruptcy within 30 days
- D) Customers who only want to buy costume jewelry
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Modern promotional financing is heavily utilized by prime and super-prime luxury buyers who understand the time-value of money and prefer to keep cash earning interest while leveraging 0% promotional terms.

#### Q10: When a customer is approved for a $12,000 credit line but only uses $7,000 for their current engagement ring purchase, what happens to the remaining $5,000?
- A) The remaining $5,000 expires in 24 hours
- B) The store must pay an MDR fee on the unused $5,000
- C) The remaining $5,000 remains available as open credit on the card for future purchases (such as wedding bands or gifts)
- D) The bank issues a cash check to the customer for $5,000
*Correct Answer:* C
*Rationale:* The unused credit line remains open on the customer's private-label revolving account, providing pre-approved purchasing power for future visits.
