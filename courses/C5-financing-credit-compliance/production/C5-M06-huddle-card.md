# Daily Floor Huddle Card: Module 6: FTC Guides & Product Representation

### 3-Minute Morning Briefing for the Floor Team

#### 1. Core Principle of the Day: The Law of the Showcase
- Every word you say about a diamond, gemstone, or gold setting is governed by federal law (FTC 16 CFR Part 23).
- **The Rule of Truth:** If a stone was grown in a factory, say **"laboratory-grown diamond."** Never say "it's a real diamond just like a mined one." Use exact, compliant terms!

#### 2. The 3 Metal Rules You Must Memorize
1. **Under 10K is NOT Gold:** In the US, an 8K or 9K piece cannot be called gold.
2. **Vermeil = Heavy Gold over Sterling:** It must have a 925 sterling base and be at least 2.5 microns thick. Gold plated over brass is NEVER vermeil!
3. **Appraisals are for Insurance, Not Resale:** Never tell a customer "this appraises for double so you're making money." Say: *"This document is prepared for your insurance company to guarantee full replacement coverage."*

#### 3. Quick Role-Play Practice Question
- *Customer:* "Is this lab-grown diamond real?"
- *Associate Response:* "It is physically, chemically, and optically a diamond, but it was created in a laboratory rather than formed in the earth over billions of years. That’s why we clearly label it a 'laboratory-grown diamond,' giving you the exact same brilliance at a significantly more accessible price point. Let's compare it side-by-side with an earth-mined diamond so you can see how both celebrate your occasion beautifully!"
