# Module 4 Production Outline: What You Cannot Promise: Approvals, Terms, Credit Scores

## 1. Executive Summary & Terminal Outcome
- **Terminal Outcome:** The fine jewelry retail professional navigates counter financing conversations within strict federal statutory boundaries, eliminating prohibited verbal promises regarding credit approvals, terms, and credit scores under the Truth in Lending Act (Regulation Z) and Equal Credit Opportunity Act (Regulation B), while handling credit declines with absolute consumer dignity and legal compliance.
- **Key Operational Competencies:**
  - Identifying and avoiding the "Seven Fatal Counter Promises" that trigger federal regulatory liability and consumer lawsuits.
  - Distinguishing soft-inquiry pre-qualification from hard-inquiry credit applications, explaining credit score impacts accurately without misrepresentation.
  - Adhering to ECOA Regulation B non-discrimination mandates regarding marital status, age, gender, and income sources.
  - Understanding Regulation Z "Trigger Terms" and mandatory counter disclosure rules.
  - Executing the standardized "Dignified Adverse Action Protocol" when an application is declined or placed in review.

## 2. Core Narrative Architecture
1. **The Legal Standing of Floor Associates:** Why retail sales associates are legally considered "agents" or "creditor representatives" under federal lending statutes; personal and store civil liability.
2. **The Seven Fatal Verbal Prohibitions:**
   - 1. "You're 100% guaranteed to be approved."
   - 2. "Applying won't hurt your credit score at all."
   - 3. "I can raise your credit limit once the manager comes over."
   - 4. "This is free money / interest-free forever."
   - 5. "We don't pull your credit report."
   - 6. "Are you married / does your husband approve of this purchase?" (ECOA Reg B violation).
   - 7. "You were declined because your credit score is too low." (FCRA violation).
3. **Credit Score Dynamics: Soft Pull vs Hard Pull:**
   - How modern pre-qualification portals (soft inquiry) differ from finalized credit line applications (hard inquiry). How to explain this difference clearly to anxious buyers.
4. **ECOA Regulation B Non-Discrimination Guardrails:**
   - Prohibited inquiries regarding marital status, childbearing intentions, or public assistance income.
   - Joint applicant rules: When and how spousal information may be collected.
5. **Truth in Lending Act (Reg Z) Trigger Terms:**
   - Why advertising "$99 a month" legally mandates disclosing the cash price, down payment, APR, and total repayment schedule.
6. **The Dignified Adverse Action Floor Protocol:**
   - Scripting the exact verbal transition when the terminal displays "Declined" or "Pending Review."
   - Preserving client self-esteem, preventing public showroom embarrassment, and pivoting to alternative payment solutions.
