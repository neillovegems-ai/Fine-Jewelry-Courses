# Module 2 Production Outline: Presenting Financing Early and Ethically

## 1. Executive Summary & Terminal Outcome
- **Terminal Outcome:** The sales associate or jewelry consultant introduces financing options naturally, early, and ethically during the discovery phase as a standard luxury concierge service, eliminating customer embarrassment, preserving price integrity, and expanding purchasing power without high-pressure or predatory tactics.
- **Key Operational Competencies:**
  - Diagnosing and dismantling associate hesitation (fear of insulting affluent clients, discomfort with credit discussions).
  - Timing the financing disclosure during the initial discovery sequence rather than as a panicked closing rescue.
  - Framing total acquisition cost in monthly cash flow terms ("around $270 per month") alongside lump-sum retail prices.
  - Delivering verbatim transition dialogues tailored to bridal couples, milestone anniversary gifters, and self-purchasers.
  - Implementing strict anti-predatory guardrails to respect customer financial boundaries.

## 2. Core Narrative Architecture
1. **The Psychology of Counter Hesitation:** Why floor associates withhold financing until a sale is collapsing; the misconception that affluent clients only pay cash.
2. **The Timing Architecture:** Mapping the four phases of the luxury sales conversation (Greeting -> Discovery -> Presentation -> Closing) and identifying the golden window for financing introduction in Discovery.
3. **The Concierge Framing Technique:** Treating financing as a premium store amenity (like custom design or private appointments) rather than a credit emergency.
4. **Monthly Payment Framing vs Aggregate Barrier:** How behavioral economics and mental accounting (Richard Thaler) explain why dividing an $8,400 investment into $350/month unlocks higher satisfaction and larger average tickets.
5. **Role-Play Scripting for Three Core Personas:** Verbatim dialogues for (a) the anxious first-time bridal buyer, (b) the anniversary upgrade spouse, and (c) the self-treating luxury collector.
6. **The Ethical Boundary Line:** Distinguishing consultative financial enablement from coercive predatory selling; recognizing budget distress signals and backing away gracefully.
