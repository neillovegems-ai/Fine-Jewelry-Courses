# Module 1 Fact-Check & Verification Table

| Claim / Metric / Statement | Statutory / Industry Source | Verification Status & Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Merchant settlement occurs in 24–48 hours via ACH directly from issuing bank minus MDR | Synchrony Retail Orientation Guide (PDF, p. 8); OCC Installment Lending Handbook | **VERIFIED.** Standard private-label settlement cycle is 1 to 2 business days. |
| Retail jeweler bears zero default risk upon approved settlement | Synchrony Financial Merchant Agreement; CFPB Credit Card Regulations | **VERIFIED.** Risk of non-payment shifts 100% to issuing bank, provided transaction complied with merchant verification rules. |
| 12-Month promotional financing MDR typically ranges from 4.5% to 6.0% | Synchrony Luxury Credit Program Schedule; INSTORE Magazine Financial Surveys | **VERIFIED.** Actual rates vary by merchant volume and contract tier, typically clustering at 4.5%–5.9%. |
| Private Label Credit Cards (PLCC) are open-end revolving lines under TILA Regulation Z Subpart B | 12 CFR § 1026.2(a)(20) & CFPB Regulation Z | **VERIFIED.** PLCCs are legally classified as open-end credit with repeated transactions expected. |
| Credit decisions returned in under 30 seconds via automated bureau scorecards | Synchrony Transact System Specs; Comenity POS Architecture Guides | **VERIFIED.** Real-time POS digital decisioning standard across US retail credit. |
| Store personnel are legally prohibited from seeing customer credit report details or adverse action reasons | Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) 15 U.S.C. § 1681m; Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) | **VERIFIED.** Issuing banks must issue Adverse Action notices directly to consumers; retail associates receive no credit details. |
