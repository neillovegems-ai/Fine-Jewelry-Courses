# Module 4 Assessment: What You Cannot Promise: Approvals, Terms, Credit Scores

### 10-Question Comprehensive Examination with Rationales

#### Q1: An associate tells a customer: "Don't worry, everyone gets approved through our jewelry financing program." Why is this statement a serious compliance violation?
- A) Because jewelry stores are not allowed to use the word "program"
- B) Because retail sales associates have zero underwriting authority, and guaranteeing credit approval is a deceptive trade practice under federal consumer protection laws
- C) Because only cash payments are legal in luxury retail
- D) Because the customer must be over 65 to be approved
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Sales associates cannot promise or guarantee credit approvals. Credit decisions are made solely by the chartered lending institution based on their underwriting algorithms.

#### Q2: Under Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) Regulation B (12 CFR § 1002.5), which of the following questions is an associate strictly PROHIBITED from asking an individual credit applicant?
- A) "What is your physical home address?"
- B) "Are you married, divorced, or single?"
- C) "What is your annual gross income?"
- D) "What is your date of birth?"
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Regulation B strictly prohibits creditors and their retail representatives from inquiring about marital status on an individual, unsecured credit application (outside community property state exemptions).

#### Q3: What is the legal difference between a "soft inquiry" and a "hard inquiry" on a customer's credit bureau profile?
- A) A soft inquiry is for small dollar amounts; a hard inquiry is for amounts over $10,000
- B) A soft inquiry (such as pre-qualification) does not impact the consumer's credit score, whereas a hard inquiry (submitting a full credit application) creates a formal inquiry on the report that may temporarily lower the score
- C) Soft inquiries are illegal under federal law
- D) Hard inquiries can only be performed by the FBI
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Soft inquiries allow consumers to view pre-qualified terms without credit score impact; final application submission triggers a hard inquiry.

#### Q4: Under Truth in Lending Act (Regulation Z), if a jeweler places a showcase card stating "Own this diamond ring for only $125 a month!", what must also be disclosed?
- A) The names of the jeweler's suppliers
- B) The full cash price, down payment requirement, terms of repayment, and the Annual Percentage Rate (APR)
- C) The jeweler's personal bank account number
- D) A disclaimer stating that jewelry is not an investment
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Stating a monthly payment amount is a "trigger term" under Regulation Z, which legally requires disclosing the cash price, down payment, complete repayment schedule, and APR.

#### Q5: If an associate attempts to convince an applicant that submitting a full, binding credit application "will not touch or affect your credit score in any way," what violation has occurred?
- A) Copyright infringement
- B) Deceptive credit representation and misrepresentation of credit reporting practices under FTC and CFPB guidelines
- C) Breach of warranty of title
- D) None; associates are encouraged to say whatever is needed to get an application
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Misrepresenting the credit score impact of a hard credit inquiry violates federal consumer protection standards prohibiting deceptive financial representations.

#### Q6: When an application is declined at the counter, what information does the POS terminal provide to the sales associate regarding the reason for the decline?
- A) The customer's exact credit score and a list of their maxed-out credit cards
- B) A statement that the customer has a history of bankruptcies
- C) None; the terminal only indicates that the application could not be approved at this time, and federal law mandates that the bank send a confidential Adverse Action notice directly to the consumer
- D) A printout showing the customer's tax returns
*Correct Answer:* C
*Rationale:* Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, credit report details are strictly confidential. Associates are not given decline reasons, ensuring customer privacy.

#### Q7: How should an associate respond if a customer asks: "Why did the bank decline me? What was wrong with my score?"
- A) "You probably have too much debt or missed some payments."
- B) "I have no way of knowing; the bank's automated system keeps that information strictly confidential to protect your privacy. They will mail a secure letter directly to your home address within 7 to 10 days with the exact details."
- C) "If you buy a smaller diamond, the bank will change their mind."
- D) "Let me call the store manager and have him override the bank's computers."
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Associates must never speculate on credit decisions. Informing the customer that the bank will mail a confidential explanation protects privacy and follows FCRA requirements.

#### Q8: Under ECOA Regulation B, can an associate require a married female applicant who qualifies for credit on her own to have her husband co-sign the application?
- A) Yes, husbands must always co-sign for jewelry purchases
- B) No, requiring a spouse to co-sign when the applicant independently meets the creditor's standards of creditworthiness is an explicit violation of federal law
- C) Only if the purchase exceeds $1,000
- D) Yes, but only in southern states
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Regulation B prohibits requiring a spouse or any co-signer if the primary applicant qualifies independently under the creditor's creditworthiness standards.

#### Q9: What is the primary operational objective of the "Dignified Adverse Action Protocol"?
- A) To force the customer to leave the store immediately
- B) To preserve the customer's self-esteem and dignity, eliminate showroom embarrassment, comply with privacy laws, and smoothly transition to alternative non-credit payment methods (such as layaway or split payments)
- C) To convince the customer to apply under their child's Social Security Number
- D) To report the customer to the local credit bureau
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* The protocol ensures luxury hospitality, maintains customer dignity, protects privacy, and preserves the sales relationship.

#### Q10: What should an associate do if a digital credit application returns a "Pending / Call Bank" status?
- A) Tell the customer they are rejected and shred their paperwork
- B) Calmly explain that the bank simply wants to verify identity for fraud prevention, call the merchant authorization line, and let the customer speak privately with the bank representative to verify their information
- C) Call the police to report identity theft
- D) Ask the customer to pay a $50 verification fee
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* "Pending / Call Bank" status usually indicates a routine fraud-prevention identity verification check that can be resolved quickly over the merchant authorization phone line.
