# Module 6 Fact-Check & Verification Table

| Claim / Metric / Statement | Statutory / Industry Source | Verification Status & Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FTC Guides define "diamond" without qualification as a natural mineral formed in the earth | 16 CFR § 23.25(a) & FTC Statement of Basis and Purpose (2018 Revisions) | **VERIFIED.** Exact federal statutory definition governing commercial diamond nomenclature. |
| Lab-grown diamonds must use "laboratory-grown", "laboratory-created", or "[Manufacturer]-created" | 16 CFR § 23.25(b) & Jewelers Vigilance Committee (JVC) Essential Guide to FTC Guidelines | **VERIFIED.** Mandatory qualifying terms enforced by FTC and JVC. |
| Minimum statutory standard for gold in the United States is 10 karats | 16 CFR § 23.3 & National Stamping Act (15 U.S.C. § 294 et seq.) | **VERIFIED.** Articles containing under 10K cannot be designated or stamped as "gold" in US retail commerce. |
| Vermeil requires sterling silver base (92.5%) coated with 10K+ gold at minimum 2.5 microns | 16 CFR § 23.4(b) (Vermeil definition) | **VERIFIED.** Explicit statutory three-part composition and thickness requirement. |
| Gemstone treatment disclosure required if non-permanent, requires special care, or affects value | 16 CFR § 23.22 (Disclosure of treatments to gemstones) | **VERIFIED.** Mandatory three-part test for trade disclosure at retail point of sale. |
| Quoting inflated appraisals as instant customer equity violates deceptive pricing rules | 16 CFR Part 233 (Guides Against Deceptive Pricing); FTC v. Various Retail Jewelers | **VERIFIED.** Misrepresenting insurance replacement valuations as actual cash value is deceptive trade practice. |
