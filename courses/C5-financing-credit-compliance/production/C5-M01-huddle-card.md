# Daily Floor Huddle Card — Module 1: How Retail Financing Works

### 3-Minute Morning Briefing for the Floor Team

#### 1. Core Principle of the Day: Financing Preserves Margin
- Offering a **10% cash discount** on a $10,000 piece throws away **$1,000** in gross cash profit.
- Offering a **12-Month 0% Promo Plan** costs the store ~**$500** in bank fees (5% MDR).
- **The Result:** The store keeps **$500 more gross profit**, preserves brand prestige, and gives the client an affordable ~$833/month payment plan!

#### 2. The 3 Counter Truths You Must Know
1. **You Are Not Lending Money:** Synchrony Bank is lending the money. The store receives 100% of the funds in 24–48 hours. If the customer doesn't pay, the bank absorbs the loss, not you or the store.
2. **Revolving Credit Compounds Your Client Book:** When a client opens our private-label card, they get a dedicated jewelry line. When you call them for their 1st anniversary, their approved line is already sitting there waiting!
3. **Zero PII on Floor Countertops:** Never write down a client's Social Security Number on scrap paper. Send the secure QR code or text link directly to their smartphone so they apply in total privacy.

#### 3. Quick Role-Play Practice Question
- *Customer:* "Do you guys offer a discount if I pay cash today?"
- *Associate Response:* "We price our fine jewelry competitively from day one to ensure exceptional value. While we don't discount our fine diamonds, what we do offer that our clients love is our 12-month interest-free payment plan. It lets you keep your $8,000 cash working in your high-yield savings or investments, while you take the ring home today for just about $665 a month. Shall we see what your private credit line looks like?"
