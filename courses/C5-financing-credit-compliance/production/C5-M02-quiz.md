# Module 2 Assessment: Presenting Financing Early and Ethically

### 10-Question Comprehensive Examination with Rationales

#### Q1: At what point in the luxury fine jewelry sales conversation is it most effective to introduce the availability of promotional financing?
- A) In the first 10 seconds before greeting the customer
- B) During Stage 2 Discovery, while discussing occasion, styles, and budget preferences
- C) Only after the customer has walked out the front door
- D) After the customer explicitly says they cannot afford to shop in your store
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Introducing financing casually during Discovery establishes it as a standard concierge amenity, normalizing payment flexibility before specific price tags trigger defensive sticker shock.

#### Q2: What is the primary psychological reason why affluent, high-income consumers choose to use 0% promotional financing?
- A) They have no cash in their bank accounts
- B) Capital preservation and the opportunity cost of money, keeping their liquid capital earning interest or dividends in investments while borrowing at 0%
- C) They want to damage their credit scores
- D) They do not understand how interest works
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Sophisticated consumers recognize that liquidating interest-bearing investments costs them money; leveraging 0% promotional financing keeps their capital working for them.

#### Q3: What is "Dual-Pricing Presentation"?
- A) Charging two different prices depending on whether the customer is dressed well
- B) Quoting both the total retail price and the estimated monthly promotional allocation simultaneously
- C) Giving one price for natural diamonds and another for lab-grown diamonds
- D) Charging an extra hidden fee at the register
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Dual-pricing states the total retail price followed immediately by the monthly breakdown (e.g., "$8,400, or about $700 a month on our 12-month interest-free plan"), anchoring the client to manageable monthly cash flow.

#### Q4: Why is presenting financing as a "last-resort rescue" when a customer balks at price considered poor salesmanship?
- A) Because the bank will automatically decline the customer
- B) Because it makes the client feel embarrassed, implies financial inadequacy, and can feel predatory
- C) Because jewelry stores are not allowed to mention financing twice
- D) Because cash discounts are always more profitable for the jeweler
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Waiting until a customer balks at price turns financing into an embarrassing compromise rather than an empowering planning tool.

#### Q5: According to Richard Thaler's Nobel-prize-winning theory of Mental Accounting, how does financing facilitate luxury purchases?
- A) It forces consumers to spend all their emergency cash
- B) It shifts the purchase from the consumer's sacred "Emergency Savings" mental bucket into their recurring "Monthly Operating Income" bucket
- C) It eliminates the need for consumers to track their monthly budget
- D) It proves that money has no objective value
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Mental accounting demonstrates that consumers guard their liquid savings fiercely; financing allows them to absorb the purchase through predictable monthly income streams without guilt.

#### Q6: An associate says: "If you really loved your fiancée, you wouldn't let an extra $150 a month stand between her and this ring." How should this sales behavior be classified?
- A) Excellent consultative closing
- B) A standard luxury sales practice
- C) Unethical, manipulative, and predatory sales conduct that violates professional standards
- D) Required under Truth in Lending regulations
*Correct Answer:* C
*Rationale:* Using emotional guilt or romantic manipulation to push debt is predatory, unprofessional, and damages customer trust and brand reputation.

#### Q7: When a customer firmly says, "No thank you, I never use credit cards; I only pay cash," how should the associate respond?
- A) Argue with the customer about the mathematical benefits of 0% interest
- B) Graciously accept their preference immediately and focus entirely on cash/direct payment options
- C) Refuse to show them any more jewelry
- D) Secretly submit an application in the background
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Ethical consultative selling respects client autonomy. Never pressure or argue with a client who prefers to pay cash.

#### Q8: What is the "Split-Payment Strategy" at the counter?
- A) Splitting the cost equally between two different customers
- B) Combining an immediate cash or debit down payment from discretionary savings with promotional financing for the remaining balance
- C) Paying half in diamonds and half in gold
- D) Splitting the invoice across two different calendar years
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* A split payment allows the client to invest the cash they initially allocated while financing the upgrade margin interest-free, achieving their dream piece without exceeding their immediate liquid comfort level.

#### Q9: Which of the following phrases is the best example of a consultative, non-presumptive financing introduction?
- A) "You look like you might need help paying for this."
- B) "Along with custom design and complimentary sizing, we offer a wonderful 12-month interest-free program that many of our clients love to keep their savings liquid."
- C) "If your credit is good enough, we can finance this."
- D) "We don't accept debit cards for items over $5,000, so you have to finance."
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* This phrasing positions financing as a premier store service alongside custom design and sizing, utilizes positive social proof, and avoids any negative assumptions about the client's financial status.

#### Q10: Why does early presentation of financing protect the store's gross profit margin?
- A) It allows the store to charge 40% interest to the customer
- B) It prevents the customer from demanding a steep 10% to 15% cash discount, preserving pricing integrity and netting higher profit even after absorbing the bank's MDR fee
- C) It forces the customer to buy extended warranties
- D) The bank pays the store a cash commission for every applicant
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Providing affordable monthly terms satisfies the customer's budget constraints, preventing the need to surrender large cash discounts and preserving brand equity and net gross margin.
