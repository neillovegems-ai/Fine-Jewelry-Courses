# Module 1 Production Outline: How Retail Financing Actually Works

## 1. Executive Summary & Terminal Outcome
- **Terminal Outcome:** The fine jewelry sales associate, bridal specialist, or store manager understands the mechanical, financial, and legal anatomy of consumer retail financing, specifically Private Label Credit Cards (PLCC) issued by financial institutions like Synchrony Bank, Comenity (Bread Financial), and Wells Fargo.
- **Key Operational Competencies:**
  - Dissecting the tripartite relationship: Consumer, Retail Jeweler, and Issuing Bank.
  - Explaining the Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) and dealer fees absorbed by the retailer to fund 0% APR or deferred-interest promotions.
  - Distinguishing open-end revolving credit lines from closed-end installment loans.
  - Operating POS digital application portals (Synchrony Transact, mobile QR code direct-to-device application) with strict data privacy.
  - Navigating primary vs secondary credit waterfalls without exposing client embarrassment.

## 2. Core Narrative Architecture
1. **The Modern Jewelry Counter Dilemma:** Why high-ticket luxury cannot survive on cash and debit alone; expanding purchasing power from disposable cash-on-hand to planned monthly cash flow.
2. **The Tripartite Credit Triangle:**
   - The Consumer: Wants immediate acquisition, preservation of emergency liquidity, predictable payments.
   - The Retail Jeweler: Gets immediate gross revenue settlement within 24–48 hours, absorbs the MDR fee, transfers credit default risk entirely to the bank.
   - The Issuing Bank: Underwrites the consumer risk, finances the receivable, earns interchange/MDR from merchant and interest/fees from revolving balances.
3. **The Economics of Promotional Financing & Merchant Discount Rates (MDR):**
   - The myth of "free money": How 6-month, 12-month, 24-month, and 36-month promotions cost the jeweler between 1.5% and 12.9% in dealer fees.
   - Margin math: Why absorbing a 4.5% MDR on an $8,500 diamond engagement ring is vastly superior to offering an 8% cash discount.
4. **Revolving Credit Lines vs Closed-End Installment Loans:**
   - Why revolving lines are the ultimate clienteling asset: creating a dedicated line of credit exclusively for fine jewelry that stimulates repeat anniversary, birthday, and holiday transactions.
5. **The POS Digital Application Workflow:**
   - Shift from paper applications (high security/identity theft risk) to customer-owned mobile screen applications via text link or QR code.
   - Instant decisioning algorithms: FICO scores, debt-to-income (DTI), bureau pulls, and security safeguards.
6. **Secondary Financing Cascades (The Waterfall):**
   - Managing near-prime and subprime second-look lenders without consumer friction.
