# Module 5 Assessment: Layaway, Installments and BNPL

### 10-Question Comprehensive Examination with Rationales

#### Q1: What is the fundamental legal difference regarding physical possession between Store Layaway and Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL)?
- A) In Layaway, the customer takes the jewelry home immediately; in BNPL, the store keeps it in the vault
- B) In Layaway, the store retains physical custody in the vault until the final payment is made; in BNPL, the customer takes physical possession immediately upon transaction execution
- C) There is no difference; both require the store to hold the piece
- D) Layaway is illegal for items over $1,000
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Layaway is a bailment agreement where delivery is deferred until full payoff; BNPL provides immediate consumer possession with installment repayment managed by the fintech lender.

#### Q2: In May 2024, what major regulatory ruling did the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) issue regarding Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) lenders?
- A) The CFPB banned all BNPL transactions in luxury retail
- B) The CFPB issued an interpretive rule classifying BNPL lenders as credit card issuers under Truth in Lending Act (Regulation Z), granting consumers dispute and refund rights
- C) The CFPB made all BNPL financing completely interest-free by law
- D) The CFPB required BNPL companies to mail physical plastic cards to all users
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* The CFPB's May 2024 interpretive rule affirmed that BNPL providers must comply with core Regulation Z open-end credit rules, including billing dispute investigations and refund rights.

#### Q3: What is the primary operational trade-off for a jeweler offering modern BNPL platforms (Affirm, Klarna) compared to traditional store layaway?
- A) Layaway costs high bank fees; BNPL is completely free to the jeweler
- B) BNPL carries a higher merchant discount fee (typically 5% to 8.5%) but delivers immediate cash settlement and immediate closing, whereas layaway costs zero fees but locks up store inventory and capital for months
- C) BNPL takes 90 days to settle funds to the store
- D) The store must repossess BNPL items if the customer defaults
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Jewelers accept higher BNPL fees to capture instant sales and rapid ACH settlement, avoiding the capital lockup and vault administration of traditional layaway.

#### Q4: If a customer defaults or cancels a traditional in-house layaway agreement, can the jeweler legally keep 100% of all payments made under all circumstances?
- A) Yes, all layaway payments are always automatically forfeited
- B) No; state layaway statutes strictly govern allowable cancellation and restocking fees, often requiring refunds of remaining deposits minus documented administrative caps
- C) Only if the item was gold
- D) Yes, but only in New York
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Consumer protection laws in numerous states prohibit retaining entire layaway balances upon cancellation, capping restocking fees and mandating refunds.

#### Q5: When a luxury jeweler underwrites a high-value customer directly using an in-house Closed-End Retail Installment Sales Contract (RISC), what federal disclosure box is legally required?
- A) The FTC Diamond Treatment Box
- B) The TILA Regulation Z "Fed Box" detailing Amount Financed, Finance Charge, APR, Total of Payments, and Payment Schedule
- C) The IRS Form 8300 cash box
- D) A standard insurance appraisal certificate
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Under 12 CFR § 1026.17 - 18, closed-end consumer credit contracts must feature the prominent Truth in Lending disclosure box containing these specific statutory figures.

#### Q6: How does a retail jeweler perfect a legal security interest against a $50,000 jewelry piece financed via an in-house installment contract?
- A) By keeping the customer's driver's license in the vault
- B) By establishing a Purchase Money Security Interest (PMSI) and filing a Form UCC-1 Financing Statement with the Secretary of State under UCC Article 9
- C) By verbally telling the customer that the store owns the jewelry
- D) By putting a GPS tracking chip inside the diamond
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* A UCC-1 filing perfects the jeweler's security interest against third parties and bankruptcy trustees under Uniform Commercial Code Article 9.

#### Q7: Why do many Gen Z and younger Millennial luxury shoppers prefer BNPL solutions over traditional private-label credit cards?
- A) Because BNPL requires physical paper checks
- B) They perceive BNPL as a structured, finite installment plan that avoids the open-ended revolving debt and retroactive interest risks of traditional credit cards
- C) BNPL only works for costume jewelry
- D) Because credit cards are illegal for consumers under 30
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Younger consumers favor finite, predictable payment schedules that do not carry open-ended compound interest or retroactive traps.

#### Q8: What happens to the jeweler's funds if a customer subsequently defaults on their payments to Affirm or Klarna after buying a diamond necklace?
- A) The jeweler must refund the money to Affirm/Klarna immediately
- B) The sales associate's commission is docked
- C) Nothing; the BNPL provider assumes 100% of the default credit risk once approved, and the jeweler retains their net settlement
- D) The store must file a police report against the customer
*Correct Answer:* C
*Rationale:* Third-party fintech BNPL providers assume all credit and default risks once the transaction is authorized, protecting the merchant's revenue.

#### Q9: What should an in-house layaway envelope contain when placed into the jewelry store vault?
- A) Only the customer's cash
- B) The jewelry piece, detailed description with carat/metal specs, laboratory certificate number, customer contact info, payment receipt history, and contract expiration date
- C) An empty jewelry box
- D) The store manager's personal keys
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* Rigorous dual-custody vault discipline requires that layaway envelopes contain full merchandise documentation, serial/cert numbers, and payment records.

#### Q10: A bridal client wants a $5,000 ring today but says: "I don't have a credit card and I don't want to open one, but I have steady income and want to pay it over the next three months." Which solution is ideal?
- A) Turn the customer away immediately
- B) Offer a mobile-native BNPL installment solution (e.g., Affirm or Klarna) that breaks the total into predictable payments with instant digital verification
- C) Tell them to borrow cash from family
- D) Force them to apply for a revolving card anyway
*Correct Answer:* B
*Rationale:* BNPL directly addresses the needs of credit-averse consumers who desire immediate possession and predictable, short-term installment repayment.
