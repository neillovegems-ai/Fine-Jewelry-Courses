# Module 4 Fact-Check & Verification Table

| Claim / Metric / Statement | Statutory / Industry Source | Verification Status & Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Truth in Lending Act (Regulation Z) mandates full disclosure when trigger terms are used in credit advertising | 12 CFR § 1026.24 (Open-End Advertising) & 12 CFR § 1026.16 | **VERIFIED.** Quoting payment amounts, down payments, or terms requires full APR and repayment disclosures. |
| Equal Credit Opportunity Act (Regulation B) prohibits inquiring about applicant marital status on individual open-end credit | 12 CFR § 1002.5(d)(1) (Rules concerning requests for information) | **VERIFIED.** Creditors and merchants are strictly forbidden from asking marital status except in community property states. |
| Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) mandates written Adverse Action notices to declined applicants | 15 U.S.C. § 1681m & CFPB Regulation B (12 CFR § 1002.9) | **VERIFIED.** Adverse action letters stating specific reasons and bureau contact info must be sent directly to the consumer. |
| Retail associates have no legal access to consumer credit report details or credit scores during POS financing | Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) § 604 & GLBA Privacy Safeguards | **VERIFIED.** POS terminals only return approval/decline/pending status; credit report details remain confidential with the bank. |
| Soft credit inquiries used for pre-qualification do not impact consumer FICO scores | Fair Isaac Corporation (FICO) Guidelines; CFPB Credit Inquiries Advisory | **VERIFIED.** Soft pulls do not impact credit scores; hard inquiries for full credit applications register as inquiries. |
