# Module 5 Production Outline: Layaway, Installments and BNPL

## 1. Executive Summary & Terminal Outcome
- **Terminal Outcome:** The fine jewelry retail professional navigates and explains alternative payment architectures—including traditional in-house layaway, modern Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) fintech platforms (Affirm, Klarna), and closed-end retail installment sales contracts (RISC)—balancing merchant fees, inventory custody, legal disclosures, and customer preferences.
- **Key Operational Competencies:**
  - Contrasting the legal mechanics of physical bailment (layaway custody) with immediate possession credit (BNPL and installment loans).
  - Evaluating the merchant cost structure (take rates ranging from 0% on layaway to 6%–9% on BNPL) against closing velocity and average order value.
  - Applying CFPB May 2024 regulatory rules classifying BNPL products as credit cards under Regulation Z (dispute rights, statement disclosures).
  - Drafting legally enforceable layaway contracts with compliant cancellation, refund, and restocking fee clauses.
  - Executing Uniform Commercial Code (UCC-1) Purchase Money Security Interests (PMSI) for high-value in-house installment contracts.

## 2. Core Narrative Architecture
1. **The Modern Payment Spectrum:** Beyond revolving private-label cards; how consumers increasingly divide into traditional cash-savers, credit card revolvers, and fintech installment users.
2. **Traditional In-House Layaway:**
   - The Bailment Model: Customer pays in installments; store holds the piece in the vault. Zero bank fees, zero interest.
   - The Hidden Merchant Costs: Holding capital in the safe for 6 months, metal/diamond market price changes, and default/abandonment management.
   - State Layaway Laws: Required written disclosures, deposit refund rules, and maximum permissible restocking fees.
3. **The Rise of Fintech Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL):**
   - Mechanics of Affirm, Klarna, and Afterpay at the luxury counter.
   - Why younger Millennial and Gen Z luxury buyers prefer BNPL (fear of revolving credit card traps).
   - Merchant Economics: Why jewelers gladly pay 6.0% to 8.5% MDR to capture high-margin impulse bridal and fashion sales.
4. **Regulatory Convergence: CFPB May 2024 BNPL Interpretive Rule:**
   - How the CFPB brought BNPL under Truth in Lending Act (Regulation Z) Subpart B, granting consumers credit card-style billing dispute rights and fee protections.
5. **Closed-End Retail Installment Sales Contracts (RISC):**
   - When independent guild jewelers finance in-house; the required federal TILA "Fed Box" disclosures (Amount Financed, Finance Charge, Total of Payments, APR).
   - Perfecting security interests under UCC Article 9.
6. **The Counter Vehicle Selection Matrix:** Matching customer psychology to the right payment method.
