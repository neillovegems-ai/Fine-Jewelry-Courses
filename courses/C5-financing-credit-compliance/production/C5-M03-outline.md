# Module 3 Production Outline: Deferred Interest, Promo Periods and Plain-Language Explanations

## 1. Executive Summary & Terminal Outcome
- **Terminal Outcome:** The fine jewelry sales professional masters the legal, mechanical, and mathematical reality of Deferred Interest financing programs, articulates the critical difference between Deferred Interest and true 0% APR, protects consumers from the catastrophic "retroactive interest trap," and provides compliant, transparent plain-language disclosures at the counter.
- **Key Operational Competencies:**
  - Defining the statutory mechanics of "No Interest if Paid in Full" (Deferred Interest) under CFPB and CARD Act regulations.
  - Calculating retroactive finance charges assessed when a consumer leaves even $1.00 unpaid at the conclusion of a promotional period.
  - Exposing the "Minimum Payment Trap": Explaining why paying only the minimum billing statement amount guarantees promotional default.
  - Applying the "N - 1 Rule" to calculate a safe monthly payoff schedule that eliminates processing lag risks.
  - Delivering verbatim, non-technical plain-language disclosures that build deep consumer trust and eliminate post-purchase shock.

## 2. Core Narrative Architecture
1. **The Most Dangerous Phrase at the Counter:** "No Interest for 12 Months" vs "No Interest IF Paid in Full." Why precision in vocabulary is a legal and moral imperative.
2. **The Anatomy of Deferred Interest:**
   - How the bank calculates finance charges in the background every single month at the standard purchase APR (28.99%–34.99%).
   - The conditional waiver: If the principal reaches exactly $0.00 before the promotional expiration date, all accrued interest is forgiven.
   - The trigger event: If a single dollar remains on Day 366, 100% of the accrued interest from the original purchase date is billed in a single lump sum.
3. **The Minimum Payment Trap:**
   - Federal regulations require credit card issuers to state a minimum monthly payment (typically ~2.5% to 3.0% of balance).
   - Mathematical proof: Showing how paying the minimum on a $5,000, 12-month deferred purchase leaves an unpaid balance of over $3,200 at month 12, triggering over $900 in retroactive penalties.
4. **Deferred Interest vs True 0% APR / Equal Payment Promotions:**
   - Comparing promotional structures: Waived Interest vs Equal Monthly Payments vs Reduced APR (9.99%).
5. **The Counter Payoff Formula (The N - 1 Rule):**
