# Daily Floor Huddle Card — Module 5: Layaway, Installments & BNPL

### 3-Minute Morning Briefing for the Floor Team

#### 1. Core Principle of the Day: The Custody Rule
- **Layaway = The Store Holds the Diamond.** The piece stays locked in our safe until the very last dollar is paid.
- **BNPL & Credit Cards = The Customer Wears the Diamond Today.** The customer takes the jewelry home immediately; the fintech lender or bank pays our store in 24–48 hours.

#### 2. Matching Payment Methods to Buyer Personas
- If a client has the proposal planned for 6 months from now and hates credit cards: **Offer In-House Layaway.**
- If a younger client wants the ring today, has a debit card, but dislikes revolving cards: **Offer BNPL (Affirm/Klarna).**
- If a client wants the ring today and plans to buy matching bands and anniversary gifts later: **Offer Synchrony Revolving Card.**

#### 3. Quick Role-Play Practice Question
- *Customer:* "I don't like credit cards, but I don't want to spend $4,000 all at once today."
- *Associate Response:* "We have two wonderful options that our clients love! If your event is a few months away, we can put the piece in our complimentary in-house layaway with zero interest and zero credit checks. Or, if you'd like to take it home tonight, we offer Affirm, which lets you split the purchase into fixed monthly payments right from your phone. Which timeline feels best for you?"
